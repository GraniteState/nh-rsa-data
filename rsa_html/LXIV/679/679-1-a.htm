TITLE LXIV
PLANNING AND ZONING

CHAPTER 679
HOUSING APPEALS BOARD

Section 679:1-a

    679:1-a Housing Appeals Board; Administrative Attachment. – The housing appeals board shall be administratively attached to the board of tax and land appeals for budgetary and administrative purposes, in accordance with RSA 21-G:10. Staff for each board may provide support for either board, as is deemed necessary by each board.

Source. 2025, 141:336, eff. July 1, 2025.
