TITLE LXIV
PLANNING AND ZONING

CHAPTER 673
LOCAL LAND USE BOARDS

Abolition of Boards or Zoning Ordinances

Section 673:22

    673:22 Transfer of Documents Upon Abolition of Zoning Ordinance. –
I. Whenever a zoning ordinance is abolished, the records of the zoning board of adjustment shall be transferred to the planning board.
II. [Repealed.]

Source. 1983, 447:1, eff. Jan. 1, 1984. 2026, 318:28; 64, III, eff. July 1, 2026 at 12:01 a.m.
