TITLE XXXVII
INSURANCE

CHAPTER 417-F
COVERAGE FOR EMERGENCY SERVICES

Section 417-F:1

    417-F:1 Definitions. –
In this chapter:
I. "Emergency medical condition" means "emergency medical condition" as defined in RSA 420-J:3, XV.
II. "Emergency services" means "emergency services" as defined in RSA 420-J:3, XVI.
III. "Insurer" means any entity providing managed care coverage or accident or health insurance or accident and health insurance policies, contracts, certificates, or other evidence of coverage to enrollees, insureds, or subscribers pursuant to RSA 415, 415-A, 420-A, 420-B, or 420-J.

Source. 1997, 134:1. 2000, 104:3, eff. July 7, 2000. 2018, 356:5, eff. July 1, 2018. 2023, 240:4, eff. Aug. 8, 2023. 2024, 143:8, eff. Jan. 1, 2025.
