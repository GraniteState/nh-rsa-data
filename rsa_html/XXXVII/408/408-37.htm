TITLE XXXVII
INSURANCE

CHAPTER 408
LIFE INSURANCE

Variable Products

Section 408:37

    408:37 False Statement. –
I. Whoever violates any provisions of RSA 408:26-36, inclusive, or knowingly files with the commissioner, or furnishes to him or her any false or misleading statements or information, shall be guilty of a class B felony, if a natural person, or guilty of a felony if any other person.
II. Any false or misleading statements or information furnished to the commissioner shall be evidence that any sales of such variable products made thereafter were made on false representation in a suit to recover damages on account of loss sustained through the purchase thereof.

Source. 2022, 144:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2023.
