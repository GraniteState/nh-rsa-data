TITLE XXXVII
INSURANCE

CHAPTER 408
LIFE INSURANCE

Annuities

Section 408:20

    408:20 Group Annuities Defined. – No group annuity may be delivered or issued for delivery in this state to a group which does not meet the definition of group annuity as defined in rules. The commissioner may promulgate rules defining groups for annuities.

Source. 2022, 144:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2023.
