TITLE XXXVII
INSURANCE

CHAPTER 407-E
GRANITE STATE HOME MITIGATION AND RESILIENCY PROGRAM

Section 407-E:9

    407-E:9 Payment of Grant Funds. –
I. Approved grants shall only be paid after the residential real property owner establishes that the applicable work has been completed.
II. The residential real property owner establishes that the non-IBHS work has been completed by providing the department with:
(a) A copy of the signed contract between the eligible contractor and the real property owner for the work.
(b) A copy of the itemized invoice detailing the work.
(c) A sworn affidavit from the eligible contractor who performed the work that the work has been completed in conformance with the requirements of this chapter.
III. In addition to the requirements of paragraph II, for IBHS grants, the residential property owner shall further provide the department with:
(a) The IBHS evaluator's required interim inspection report during construction.
(b) A FORTIFIED Home roof or silver designation certificate.
IV. If the residential real property owner has established compliance with this section, the department shall issue the approved grant funds directly to the eligible contractor who performed the work for the residential real property owner.
V. The real property owner is responsible for any amount owed to an eligible contractor that exceeds the amount of the awarded grant moneys.

Source. 2026, 265:1, eff. Aug. 31, 2026.
