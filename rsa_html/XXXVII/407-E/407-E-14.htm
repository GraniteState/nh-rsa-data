TITLE XXXVII
INSURANCE

CHAPTER 407-E
GRANITE STATE HOME MITIGATION AND RESILIENCY PROGRAM

Section 407-E:14

    407-E:14 Appeals; Hearing. – Any person aggrieved by any act of the commissioner under this chapter may file a request for hearing pursuant to RSA 400-A:17, II(b).

Source. 2026, 265:1, eff. Aug. 31, 2026.
