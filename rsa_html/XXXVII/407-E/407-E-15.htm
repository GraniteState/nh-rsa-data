TITLE XXXVII
INSURANCE

CHAPTER 407-E
GRANITE STATE HOME MITIGATION AND RESILIENCY PROGRAM

Section 407-E:15

    407-E:15 Rulemaking. – The commissioner may adopt rules under RSA 541-A that are reasonable and necessary for the implementation and administration of this chapter.

Source. 2026, 265:1, eff. Aug. 31, 2026.
