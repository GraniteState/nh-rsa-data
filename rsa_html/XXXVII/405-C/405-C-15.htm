TITLE XXXVII
INSURANCE

CHAPTER 405-C
MULTIPLE-CAREGIVER SELF-INSURED RISK COVERAGE ARRANGEMENTS

Section 405-C:15


[RSA 405-C:15 effective July 1, 2027.]
    405-C:15 Rehabilitation, Dissolution. – Any rehabilitation, liquidation, conservation, or dissolution of an arrangement shall be conducted under the supervision of the commissioner, who shall have all power with respect thereto granted to it under the laws governing the rehabilitation, liquidation, conservation, or dissolution of insurers.

Source. 2026, 273:1, eff. July 1, 2027.
