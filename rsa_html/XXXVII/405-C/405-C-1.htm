TITLE XXXVII
INSURANCE

CHAPTER 405-C
MULTIPLE-CAREGIVER SELF-INSURED RISK COVERAGE ARRANGEMENTS

Section 405-C:1


[RSA 405-C:1 effective July 1, 2027.]
    405-C:1 Purpose. – This chapter provides authority for 2 or more nonprofit and for-profit providers and servicers of child care, day care, foster care placement, and behavioral health services to participate in a joint self-insurance risk coverage arrangement covering liability risks. The joint self-insurance arrangements authorized under this chapter permit eligible entities to jointly self-insure liability risks, jointly purchase insurance and reinsurance, and contract for risk management, claims, and administrative and legal services.

Source. 2026, 273:1, eff. July 1, 2027.
