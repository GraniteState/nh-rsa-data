TITLE XXXVII
INSURANCE

CHAPTER 405-C
MULTIPLE-CAREGIVER SELF-INSURED RISK COVERAGE ARRANGEMENTS

Section 405-C:17


[RSA 405-C:17 effective July 1, 2027.]
    405-C:17 Rulemaking Authority. – The commissioner may adopt rules, pursuant to RSA 541-A, as may be necessary to effect the purposes of this chapter.

Source. 2026, 273:1, eff. July 1, 2027.
