TITLE XXXVII
INSURANCE

CHAPTER 420-D
CONTINUING CARE COMMUNITIES

Section 420-D:11

    420-D:11 Pledging Assets. – As long as the provider is in compliance with New Hampshire law and subject to the approval requirement of section RSA 420-D:2, unencumbered assets of a facility may be pledged as collateral. Notice shall be given to the commissioner of any additional liens.

Source. 2025, 296:2, eff. Jan. 1, 2026.
