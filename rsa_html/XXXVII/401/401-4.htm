TITLE XXXVII
INSURANCE

CHAPTER 401
INCORPORATION AND POWERS OF INSURANCE COMPANIES

Section 401:4

    401:4 Paid-up Capital. – All stock companies organized on or after January 1, 2026, under the provisions of this chapter shall have a paid-up capital of at least $3,000,000. Stock companies organized prior to January 1, 2026, shall have and at all times maintain paid up capital of not less than the minimum capital required for the point in time at which the company was organized.

Source. 1917, 80:4. PL 272:4. RL 322:4. 1947, 182:3. RSA 401:4. 1965, 51:1. 1969, 283:1, eff. June 25, 1969. 2025, 191:10, eff. Sept. 13, 2025.
