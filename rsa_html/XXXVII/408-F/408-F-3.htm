TITLE XXXVII
INSURANCE

CHAPTER 408-F
LIFE AND HEALTH INSURANCE GUARANTY ASSOCIATION OF 2019

Section 408-F:3

    408-F:3 Construction. – This chapter shall be liberally construed to effect the purpose under RSA 408-F:2 which shall constitute an aid and guide to interpretation.

Source. 2019, 314:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2020.
