TITLE X
PUBLIC HEALTH

CHAPTER 126-Z
RIGHT TO TRY ACT

Section 126-Z:9


[RSA 126-Z:9 effective upon certified approval of a legislative report regarding the amendment of the Right to Try Act pursuant to 2026, 311:21.]
    126-Z:9 Rulemaking. – The commissioner of the department of health and human services shall adopt, under RSA 541-A, any rules necessary to implement this chapter.

Source. 2026, 311:19, eff. contingent.
