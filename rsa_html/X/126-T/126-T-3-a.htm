TITLE X
PUBLIC HEALTH

CHAPTER 126-T
COMMISSION ON THE INTERDISCIPLINARY PRIMARY CARE WORKFORCE ISSUES

Section 126-T:3-a


[RSA 126-T:3-a repealed by 2010, 114:3, effective November 1, 2029.]
    126-T:3-a Meetings Open to Public and Quorum Requirement. – Notwithstanding RSA 91-A:2, IV, or any other law to the contrary, at least one-seventh of the total appointed membership of the commission shall be present at the physical location of the meeting. Each member participating electronically or otherwise shall be able to contemporaneously and throughout the meeting see and hear, and be seen and heard by, the other members of the public body attending the meeting and members of the public in attendance at the meeting site. A member participating in a meeting remotely as described in this paragraph is deemed to be present for all purposes, including for determination of a quorum and voting. Each member participating remotely shall identify the persons present in the location from which the member is participating. All votes taken during such a meeting shall be by roll call vote. Members of the public shall be permitted to participate remotely in remotely held commission meetings, including testifying or asking questions as the rules and procedures of the commission allow. A majority of the appointed members of the commission shall constitute a quorum.

Source. 2025, 122:3, eff. June 24, 2025.
