TITLE X
PUBLIC HEALTH

CHAPTER 125-S
MOTOR VEHICLE AIR POLLUTION ABATEMENT FUND

Section 125-S:2


[RSA 125-S:2 effective until satisfaction of conditions specified by 2025, 141:255; see also RSA 125-S:2 effective upon satisfaction of conditions specified by 2025, 141:255.]
    125-S:2 Definitions. –
In this chapter:
I. "Department" means the department of environmental services.
II. "Motor vehicle inspection fee" means the fee collected by the department of safety pursuant to RSA 266:2.
III. "Mobile source" means, for the purposes of this chapter, any motor vehicle registered for on-road use by the department of safety, division of motor vehicles.
[RSA 125-S:2 effective contingent upon satisfaction of conditions specified by 2025, 141:255; see also RSA 125-S:2 set out above.]
