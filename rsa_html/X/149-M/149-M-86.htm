TITLE X
PUBLIC HEALTH

CHAPTER 149-M
SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT

Solid Waste Site Evaluation Committee

Section 149-M:86


[RSA 149-M:86 effective Sept. 8, 2026.]
    149-M:86 Solid Waste Permit Applications Suspended. – The department shall not issue any permit approvals that authorize new capacity for major solid waste disposal facilities until rules are adopted by the committee or until July 1, 2027, whichever is later.

Source. 2026, 288:1, eff. Sept. 8, 2026.
