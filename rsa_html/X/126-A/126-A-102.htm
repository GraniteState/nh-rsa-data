TITLE X
PUBLIC HEALTH

CHAPTER 126-A
DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES

Data Privacy and Information Technology Security Governance Board

Section 126-A:102

    126-A:102 Data Privacy and Information Technology Security Governance Board Established. – There is hereby established a data privacy and information technology security governance board to oversee the department's use of data, data privacy, and information technology security that shall be maintained by the department of health and human services.

Source. 2023, 79:426, eff. July 1, 2023.
