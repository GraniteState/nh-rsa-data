TITLE XXXIV
PUBLIC UTILITIES

CHAPTER 374-G
ELECTRIC UTILITY INVESTMENT IN DISTRIBUTED ENERGY RESOURCES

Section 374-G:4

    374-G:4 Investments in Distributed Energy Resources. –
I. Notwithstanding any other provision of law to the contrary, as provided in RSA 374-G:5, a New Hampshire electric public utility may invest in or own distributed energy resources, located on or inter-connected to the local electric distribution system.
II. Distributed electric generation owned by or receiving investments from an electric utility under this section shall be limited to a cumulative maximum in megawatts of 10 percent of the utility's total distribution peak load in megawatts. This limitation shall not apply to energy storage or demand response.

Source. 2008, 373:1, eff. Sept. 9, 2008. 2021, 228:2, Pt. I, Sec. 4, eff. Oct. 25, 2021. 2026, 108:1, eff. July 27, 2026; 334:5, II, eff. Sept. 13, 2026.
