TITLE LV
PROCEEDINGS IN SPECIAL CASES

CHAPTER 545-B
UNIFORM VOIDABLE TRANSACTIONS ACT

Section 545-B:15

    545-B:15 Short Title; Predecessor References. – The predecessor of this chapter, RSA 545-A, was cited as the Uniform Fraudulent Transfer Act, and this chapter, RSA 545-B, may be cited as the Uniform Voidable Transactions Act.

Source. 2026, 215:1, eff. Aug. 31, 2026.
