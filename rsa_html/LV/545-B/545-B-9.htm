TITLE LV
PROCEEDINGS IN SPECIAL CASES

CHAPTER 545-B
UNIFORM VOIDABLE TRANSACTIONS ACT

Section 545-B:9

    545-B:9 Extinguishment of Claim for Relief. –
A claim for relief with respect to a transfer or obligation under this chapter is extinguished unless action is brought:
I. Under RSA 545-B:4, I(a), not later than 4 years after the transfer was made or the obligation was incurred or, if later, not later than one year after the transfer or obligation was or could reasonably have been discovered by the claimant;
II. Under RSA 545-B:4, I(b) or RSA 545-B:5, I, not later than 4 years after the transfer was made or the obligation was incurred; or
III. Under RSA 545-B:5, II, not later than one year after the transfer was made.

Source. 2026, 215:1, eff. Aug. 31, 2026.
