TITLE LV
PROCEEDINGS IN SPECIAL CASES

CHAPTER 545-B
UNIFORM VOIDABLE TRANSACTIONS ACT

Section 545-B:14

    545-B:14 Relation to Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act. – This chapter modifies, limits, or supersedes the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act, 15 U.S.C. Section 7001 et seq., but does not modify, limit, or supersede Section 101(c) of that act, 15 U.S.C. Section 7001(c), or authorize electronic delivery of any of the notices described in Section 103(b) of that act, 15 U.S.C. Section 7003(b).

Source. 2026, 215:1, eff. Aug. 31, 2026.
