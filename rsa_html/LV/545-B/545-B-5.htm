TITLE LV
PROCEEDINGS IN SPECIAL CASES

CHAPTER 545-B
UNIFORM VOIDABLE TRANSACTIONS ACT

Section 545-B:5

    545-B:5 Transfer or Obligation Voidable as to Present Creditor. –
I. A transfer made or obligation incurred by a debtor is voidable as to a creditor whose claim arose before the transfer was made or the obligation was incurred if the debtor made the transfer or incurred the obligation without receiving a reasonably equivalent value in exchange for the transfer or obligation and the debtor was insolvent at that time or the debtor became insolvent as a result of the transfer or obligation.
II. A transfer made by a debtor is voidable as to a creditor whose claim arose before the transfer was made if the transfer was made to an insider for an antecedent debt, the debtor was insolvent at that time, and the insider had reasonable cause to believe that the debtor was insolvent.
III. Subject to RSA 545-B:2, II, a creditor making a claim for relief under paragraph I or II has the burden of proving the elements of the claim for relief by a preponderance of the evidence.

Source. 2026, 215:1, eff. Aug. 31, 2026.
