TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 326-M
NURSE AGENCY REGISTRATION

Section 326-M:4

    326-M:4 Rulemaking. – The executive director of the office of professional licensure and certification shall adopt rules, pursuant to RSA 541-A, relative to the necessary forms, application procedures, and fees required under this chapter.

Source. 2023, 242:1, eff. Oct. 7, 2023.
