TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 316-A
CHIROPRACTIC

Section 316-A:17

    316-A:17 National Examination; Examination Requirements. – The board shall require that applicants pass parts 1, 2, and 3 of the written clinical competency examinations of the national examination given by the National Board of Chiropractic Examiners if an applicant was licensed after January 1, 1990. Applicants shall pass the ethics and jurisprudence examination given by the New Hampshire board of chiropractic examiners. Provided the applicant meets all other qualifications and requirements of this chapter, the applicant shall be registered and granted a license by the office of professional licensure and certification upon payment of the required fee and presentation of satisfactory proof that the applicant has passed the parts of such examinations.

Source. 1988, 87:2. 1995, 132:4. 2014, 322:1, eff. Sept. 30, 2014. 2024, 327:40, eff. July 1, 2024. 2026, 112:1, eff. July 27, 2026.
