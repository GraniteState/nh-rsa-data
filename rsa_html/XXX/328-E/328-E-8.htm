TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 328-E
NATUROPATHIC HEALTH CARE PRACTICE

Section 328-E:8

    328-E:8 Powers and Duties of the Board. –
I. The board shall ensure that doctors of naturopathic medicine serving the public meet minimum standards of proficiency and competency to protect the health, safety, and welfare of the public.
