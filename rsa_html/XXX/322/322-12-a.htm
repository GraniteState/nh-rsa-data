TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 322
JUNK AND SCRAP METAL DEALERS

Section 322:12-a

    322:12-a Metal Kegs for Malt Beverages. – No junk or scrap metal dealer licensed by a governing body under the authority of this chapter shall purchase any metal keg for malt beverages or pieces of such metal keg, unless the seller is the brewer whose name or marking appears on the keg. If the brewer's name or marking has been removed or obliterated, the licensee shall not purchase the keg. As used in this section, "keg" means any refillable brewery-sealed, individual container of malt beverage having a liquid capacity of more than 64 fluid ounces.

Source. 2008, 287:10, eff. June 27, 2008. 2024, 193:2, eff. Jan. 1, 2025.
