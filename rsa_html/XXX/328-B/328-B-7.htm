TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 328-B
MASSAGE THERAPISTS AND MASSAGE ESTABLISHMENTS

Section 328-B:7


[RSA 328-B:7 effective until July 1, 2027; see also RSA 328-B:7 set out below.]
    328-B:7 License Renewal. – All licenses issued pursuant to this chapter shall expire on the last day of the birth month of the licensee in the even-numbered year, upon approval of the executive director of the renewal application and submission of the required renewal fee.
[RSA 328-B:7 effective July 1, 2027; see also RSA 328-B:7 above.]
    328-B:7 License Renewal. – All licenses issued pursuant to this chapter shall expire in accordance with RSA 310:8.
