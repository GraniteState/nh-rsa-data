TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 313-A
BARBERING, COSMETOLOGY, AND ESTHETICS

Section 313-A:20

    313-A:20 Expiration and Renewal of Licenses. – All licenses established under this chapter shall expire in accordance with RSA 310:8.

Source. 2000, 118:1. 2007, 47:3. 2009, 196:2, eff. July 1, 2009. 2016, 297:7, eff. Aug. 20, 2016. 2021, 205:2, Pt. VIII, Sec. 4, eff. Oct. 9, 2021. 2024, 327:26, eff. July 1, 2024.
