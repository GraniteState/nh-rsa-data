TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 313-A
BARBERING, COSMETOLOGY, AND ESTHETICS

Section 313-A:7

    313-A:7 Duties. –
I. The board shall:
(a) Prescribe the duties of its officers;
(b) Keep a record of its proceedings in accordance with the retention policy established by the office of professional licensure and certification; and
II. The board may establish criteria pursuant to RSA 541-A to license a school to operate either:
(a) Dedicated programs within secondary schools, the purpose of which is to teach cosmetology, manicuring, barbering, or esthetics; or
(b) Postsecondary programs conducted for the purpose of teaching cosmetology, manicuring, barbering, or esthetics, including postsecondary programs leading to a certificate in manicuring, barbering, cosmetology, or esthetics.

Source. 2000, 118:1. 2004, 177:5. 2014, 132:5, eff. June 16, 2014. 2015, 276:75, eff. July 1, 2015. 2021, 197:33, 35, II, eff. July 1, 2021; 205:2, Pt. VIII, Sec. 2, eff. Oct. 9, 2021. 2024, 327:17, eff. July 1, 2024.
