TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 327
OPTOMETRY

License Renewal

Section 327:13

    327:13 Renewal of Licenses. – All licenses issued under this chapter shall be renewed every two years in accordance with RSA 310:8.

Source. 1911, 167:9. 1919, 78:2. PL 207:14. RL 253:14. 1999, 197:10. 2014, 167:35. 2015, 229:11, eff. July 1, 2015. 2024, 327:120, eff. July 1, 2024.
