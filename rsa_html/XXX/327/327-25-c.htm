TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 327
OPTOMETRY

Regulations and Penalties

Section 327:25-c

    327:25-c Telemedicine. – Persons licensed by the office of professional licensure and certification shall be permitted to provide services through the use of telemedicine, as defined in RSA 310:7.

Source. 2020, 27:21, eff. July 21, 2020. 2024, 327:123, eff. July 1, 2024.
