TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 318
PHARMACISTS AND PHARMACIES

Examinations and Licenses

Section 318:19


[RSA 318:19 effective until Sept. 8, 2026; see also RSA 318:19 set out below.]
    318:19 Examinations. – Applicants for licensure as pharmacists shall, to prove their respective requisite knowledge, be examined to a properly varying degree in pharmacy-related subject areas which may include chemistry, math, pharmacology, pharmacy theory, the practice of pharmacy and pharmacy law, and any other areas as the board may prescribe.
[RSA 318:19 effective Sept. 8, 2026; see also RSA 318:19 above.]
    318:19 Examinations. – Applicants for licensure as pharmacists shall, to prove their respective requisite knowledge, be examined to a properly varying degree in pharmacy-related subject areas which may include chemistry, math, pharmacology, pharmacy theory, the practice of pharmacy, and any other areas as the board may prescribe, except that the board shall not require any applicant to be examined on pharmacy state-specific or universal state level jurisprudence or law.

Source. 1921, 122:13. PL 210:20. RL 256:20. 1949, 280:1, 2. 1981, 484:18, 20. 1985, 324:6, eff. Jan. 1, 1986. 2026, 311:11, eff. Sept. 8, 2026.
