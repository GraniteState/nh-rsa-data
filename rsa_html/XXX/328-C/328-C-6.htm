TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 328-C
FAMILY MEDIATORS

Section 328-C:6


[RSA 328-C:6 effective until Sept. 8, 2026; see also RSA 328-C:6 set out below.]
    328-C:6 Continuing Education. – To renew a certification, each family mediator shall provide evidence of attendance within the last 3 years of at least 24 hours of continuing education approved by the board. A certified family mediator training program seeking renewal shall provide evidence that it has met any reporting requirements established under RSA 328-C:4-a and that each of the family mediators associated with the program has met the annual continuing education requirements.
[RSA 328-C:6 effective Sept. 8, 2026; see also RSA 328-C:6 above.]
    328-C:6 Continuing Education. – To renew a certification, each family mediator shall provide evidence of attendance within the last 2 years of at least 16 hours of continuing education approved by the board. A certified family mediator training program seeking renewal shall provide evidence that it has met any reporting requirements established under RSA 328-C:4-a and that each of the family mediators associated with the program has met the annual continuing education requirements.

Source. 1989, 268:1. 2005, 250:10. 2009, 21:4, eff. Jan. 1, 2010. 2026, 300:11, eff. Sept. 8, 2026.
