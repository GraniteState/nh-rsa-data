TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 325
EMBALMERS AND FUNERAL DIRECTORS

Section 325:30

    325:30 Issuance; Term; Renewal. – Apprentice licenses shall be issued for a period established by RSA 310:8. The fees for an apprentice license shall be established by the office of professional licensure and certification.

Source. 1935, 95:1. RL 168:30. 1953, 150:2. RSA 325:30. 1957, 77:6. 1973, 354:5. 1977, 563:3. 1981, 487:20. 1983, 325:4, eff. Aug. 17, 1983. 2024, 327:84, eff. July 1, 2024.
