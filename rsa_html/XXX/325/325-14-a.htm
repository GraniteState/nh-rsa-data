TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 325
EMBALMERS AND FUNERAL DIRECTORS

Section 325:14-a

    325:14-a Associate Funeral Directors. –
No person shall engage or hold himself or herself out as engaged in being an associate funeral director, unless the person:
I. Holds an associate funeral director's license from the office of professional licensure and certification;
II. Has passed such examinations approved by the board to ascertain efficiency and qualifications to engage in being an associate funeral director;
III. Has completed a funeral director certificate program approved by the board; and
IV. Has completed a one-year course in apprenticeship under the supervision of a duly licensed or registered funeral director or embalmer.

Source. 2026, 22:1, eff. June 2, 2026.
