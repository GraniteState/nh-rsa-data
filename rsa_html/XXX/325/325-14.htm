TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 325
EMBALMERS AND FUNERAL DIRECTORS

Section 325:14

    325:14 Funeral Directors; Qualifications. –
No person shall engage or hold himself or herself out as engaged in funeral directing, unless the person:
I. Is the holder of an embalmer's license;
II. Has passed such examinations approved by the board to ascertain efficiency and qualifications to engage in funeral directing; and
III. Obtains the appropriate license from the office of professional licensure and certification.

Source. 1935, 95:1. RL 168:14. RSA 325:14. 1981, 487:8, eff. July 1, 1981. 2024, 327:77, eff. July 1, 2024.
