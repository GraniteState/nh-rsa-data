TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 326-F
SPEECH-LANGUAGE PATHOLOGY AND HEARING CARE PROVIDERS PRACTICE

Section 326-F:13

    326-F:13 Unsolicited Home Sales Prohibited. – No hearing aid dealer or audiologist, employee or agent thereof, shall canvass either in person or by telephone from house to house for the purpose of selling or renting a hearing aid without prior request from the prospective customer, a relative or friend of the prospective customer.

Source. 2021, 91:183, eff. July 1, 2021.
