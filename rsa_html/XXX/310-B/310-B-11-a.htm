TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 310-B
STATE LICENSED OR CERTIFIED REAL ESTATE APPRAISERS

Section 310-B:11-a

    310-B:11-a Temporary License or Certificate. – After application and payment of a fee, the office shall issue a temporary license or certificate to an individual who is either licensed or certified in another jurisdiction if the application complies with rules adopted by the board pursuant to RSA 541-A.

Source. 2026, 53:3, eff. July 7, 2026.
