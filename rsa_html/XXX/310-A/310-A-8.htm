TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 310-A
OFFICE OF PROFESSIONAL LICENSURE AND CERTIFICATION

Professional Engineers

Section 310-A:8

    310-A:8 Disbursements. – The office may make expenditures for the reasonable expenses of the board's delegate to meetings of, and membership dues to, the National Council of Examiners for Engineering and Surveying (NCEES).

Source. 1981, 485:1. 1995, 284:10. 2013, 144:111, eff. July 1, 2013. 2015, 276:51, eff. July 1, 2015. 2024, 327:255, eff. July 1, 2024.
