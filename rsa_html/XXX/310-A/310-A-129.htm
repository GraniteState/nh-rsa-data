TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 310-A
OFFICE OF PROFESSIONAL LICENSURE AND CERTIFICATION

Professional Geologists

Section 310-A:129

    310-A:129 Examinations. – [There is no RSA 310-A:128.]
The scope of the technical and professional examination and the methods of procedure shall be prescribed by the board.

Source. 2000, 297:3. 2014, 174:12, eff. Sept. 9, 2014. 2024, 327:201, eff. July 1, 2024.
