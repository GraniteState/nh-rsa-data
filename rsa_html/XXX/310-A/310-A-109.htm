TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 310-A
OFFICE OF PROFESSIONAL LICENSURE AND CERTIFICATION

Licensing Foresters

Section 310-A:109

    310-A:109 License Renewal. – Licenses may be renewed every 2 years in accordance with RSA 310:8. The applicant shall have completed 20 hours of continuing education approved by the board at the time of license renewal, and shall submit a record of any legal action brought against the applicant for services as a forester.

Source. 1990, 166:5. 1995, 136:41, eff. July 23, 1995. 2024, 327:194, eff. July 1, 2024.
