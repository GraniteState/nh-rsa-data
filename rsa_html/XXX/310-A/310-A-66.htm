TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 310-A
OFFICE OF PROFESSIONAL LICENSURE AND CERTIFICATION

Land Surveyors

Section 310-A:66

    310-A:66 Examinations. – The board shall prescribe the examination requirements in rules adopted pursuant to RSA 541-A.
