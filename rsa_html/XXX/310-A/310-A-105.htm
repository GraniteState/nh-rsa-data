TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 310-A
OFFICE OF PROFESSIONAL LICENSURE AND CERTIFICATION

Licensing Foresters

Section 310-A:105

    310-A:105 Applications; Fees. – Applications for licensing shall contain statements made under oath as to citizenship, residence, the applicant's education, a detailed summary of the applicant's technical experience, and shall contain the names of not less than 5 references, 3 or more of whom shall be individuals having personal or professional knowledge of the applicant's forestry experience. The fee for a license as a forester shall be fixed by the office pursuant to RSA 541-A.

Source. 1990, 166:5. 1995, 136:39, eff. July 23, 1995; 284:50, eff. Jan. 1, 1996. 2024, 327:190, eff. July 1, 2024.
