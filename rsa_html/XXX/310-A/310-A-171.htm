TITLE XXX
OCCUPATIONS AND PROFESSIONS

CHAPTER 310-A
OFFICE OF PROFESSIONAL LICENSURE AND CERTIFICATION

Court Reporters

Section 310-A:171

    310-A:171 Fees. –
The executive director shall adopt rules, under RSA 541-A, which shall establish all fees required under this subdivision including fees for the following:
I. Examinations.
II. Application for licensure upon passing the examination.
III. Application for a license under RSA 310-A:170.
IV. Biennial renewal for licensed court reporters.
V. Late fees for a late renewal of license.
VI. Replacement of a lost or mutilated license.
VII. Transcribing and transferring records and other services.
VIII. Reinstatement.

Source. 2007, 300:1, eff. July 1, 2007. 2022, 64:6, eff. July 19, 2022.
