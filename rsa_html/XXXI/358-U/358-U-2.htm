TITLE XXXI
TRADE AND COMMERCE

CHAPTER 358-U
DIGITAL ASSET TRANSACTION KIOSKS

Section 358-U:2


[RSA 358-U:2 effective December 16, 2026.]
    358-U:2 Definitions. –
I. "Customer" means a natural person using a kiosk.
II. "Digital asset" means a digital representation of value recorded on a distributed ledger or blockchain, including cryptocurrency.
III. "Digital asset transaction kiosk" or "kiosk" means a self-service electronic terminal located in a public or retail setting that enables a person to buy, sell, or transfer a digital asset using cash or another payment method.
IV. "Operator" means a person or entity that owns, manages, or controls one or more kiosks in this state, whether directly or through a third-party host location.
V. "Reference price" means a real-time price for the covered digital asset derived from a public exchange or composite index.

Source. 2026, 187:2, eff. Dec. 16, 2026.
