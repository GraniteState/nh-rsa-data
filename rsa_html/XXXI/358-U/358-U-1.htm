TITLE XXXI
TRADE AND COMMERCE

CHAPTER 358-U
DIGITAL ASSET TRANSACTION KIOSKS

Section 358-U:1


[RSA 358-U:1 effective December 16, 2026.]
    358-U:1 Statement of Purpose. – It is the intent of the general court that this chapter establish reasonable and uniform consumer protections while permitting lawful and responsible digital asset transaction kiosk operations.

Source. 2026, 187:2, eff. Dec. 16, 2026.
