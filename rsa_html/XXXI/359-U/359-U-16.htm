TITLE XXXI
TRADE AND COMMERCE

CHAPTER 359-U
TRANSPORTATION NETWORK COMPANIES

Section 359-U:16

    359-U:16 Records. –
Every TNC shall maintain the following customer records:
I. Individual trip records for at least one year from the date each trip was provided.
II. TNC driver records at least until the one year anniversary of the date on which a TNC driver's customer relationship with the TNC has ended.

Source. 2023, 30:2, eff. July 1, 2023.
