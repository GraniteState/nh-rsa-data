TITLE XXXI
TRADE AND COMMERCE

CHAPTER 359-U
TRANSPORTATION NETWORK COMPANIES

Section 359-U:3

    359-U:3 TNC; Permit Required. – No person shall operate a TNC in New Hampshire without first having obtained a permit from the department, provided that any transportation network company operating in the state before the effective date of this chapter may continue to operate in the state until the department establishes a permit process and provides a reasonable period in which to apply for and obtain a permit. The department shall issue a permit to each applicant that meets the requirements for a TNC under this chapter, and pays an annual permit fee of $500 to the department. The permit shall be issued annually and shall be effective on the date of issue.

Source. 2023, 30:2, eff. July 1, 2023.
