TITLE XXXI
TRADE AND COMMERCE

CHAPTER 359-U
TRANSPORTATION NETWORK COMPANIES

Section 359-U:7

    359-U:7 Receipt. – Within a reasonable period of time following the completion of a trip, a TNC shall transmit an itemized receipt to the rider on behalf of the TNC driver.

Source. 2023, 30:2, eff. July 1, 2023.
