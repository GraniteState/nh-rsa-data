TITLE XXXI
TRADE AND COMMERCE

CHAPTER 359-U
TRANSPORTATION NETWORK COMPANIES

Section 359-U:2

    359-U:2 Not Other Carriers. –
No TNC or TNC driver is a motor carrier, nor do they provide taxicab services pursuant to RSA 266-A:2. No TNC or TNC driver shall be required to:
I. Apply for a common carrier certificate under RSA 266-A:4 or a contract carrier permit under RSA 266-A:7; or
II. Register personal vehicles under RSA 266-A:24.

Source. 2023, 30:2, eff. July 1, 2023.
