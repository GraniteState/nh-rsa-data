TITLE XXXI
TRADE AND COMMERCE

CHAPTER 359-U
TRANSPORTATION NETWORK COMPANIES

Section 359-U:13

    359-U:13 Vehicle Safety. – Prior to permitting a TNC driver to accept trip requests through the TNC's digital platform, the TNC shall ensure that the TNC driver's personal vehicle meets New Hampshire's vehicle safety requirements for private motor vehicles.

Source. 2023, 30:2, eff. July 1, 2023.
