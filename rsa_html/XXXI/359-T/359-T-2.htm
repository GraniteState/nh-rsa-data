TITLE XXXI
TRADE AND COMMERCE

CHAPTER 359-T
CARRIAGE OF HOUSEHOLD GOODS FOR HIRE BY MOTOR VEHICLE

Section 359-T:2

    359-T:2 Certificate Required. – No household goods carrier, as defined herein, shall engage in the business of transporting household goods, as defined by the department, between points in this state, unless the carrier holds a certificate issued by the department authorizing such operations. The application for such certificate shall be in accordance with the procedure provided for in RSA 359-T:3 and such certificate shall be issued or denied accordingly.

Source. 2022, 14:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2023.
