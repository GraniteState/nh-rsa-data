TITLE XXXI
TRADE AND COMMERCE

CHAPTER 359-T
CARRIAGE OF HOUSEHOLD GOODS FOR HIRE BY MOTOR VEHICLE

Section 359-T:7

    359-T:7 Transfer of Certificates and Permits. – No certificate or permit, nor any rights thereunder, shall be transferred without the approval of the department.

Source. 2022, 14:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2023.
