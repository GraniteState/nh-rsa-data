TITLE XXXI
TRADE AND COMMERCE

CHAPTER 359-V
BLOCKCHAIN BASIC LAWS

Section 359-V:4


[RSA 359-V:4 effective Sept. 8, 2026.]
    359-V:4 Civil Remedies. –
I. Any person directly affected by a violation of this chapter may petition the superior court or the blockchain dispute docket for relief.
II. The superior court may order declaratory, injunctive, or other equitable relief.
III. Any person who prevails on any claim brought pursuant to this chapter, and shows by a preponderance of the evidence that the defendant engaged in a purposeful violation of this chapter, shall be entitled to an award of reasonable attorney's fees.

Source. 2026, 286:3, eff. Sept. 8, 2026.
