TITLE XVIII
FISH AND GAME

CHAPTER 215-A
OFF HIGHWAY RECREATIONAL VEHICLES AND TRAILS

OHRV Commission

Section 215-A:45

    215-A:45 Online Payment of Fees. – The executive director shall, no later than September 1, 2025, establish any procedures that may be necessary to enable the online payment of both initial and renewal registration fees required or imposed under this chapter.

Source. 2022, 206:1, eff. Aug. 16, 2022. 2023, 79:501, eff. July 1, 2023.
