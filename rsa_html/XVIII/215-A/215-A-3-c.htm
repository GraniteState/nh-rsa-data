TITLE XVIII
FISH AND GAME

CHAPTER 215-A
OFF HIGHWAY RECREATIONAL VEHICLES AND TRAILS

Section 215-A:3-c

    215-A:3-c Connecticut Lakes Headwaters Working Forest; OHRV Trail Annual Closure Date. – Notwithstanding any conservation easement provisions or other contracts, laws, or rules to the contrary, all existing OHRV trails on the Connecticut Lakes headwaters working forest property shall remain open through the second Monday in October, which is Columbus Day.

Source. 2025, 261:1, eff. July 31, 2025.
