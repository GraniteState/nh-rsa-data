TITLE XVIII
FISH AND GAME

CHAPTER 208
GAME ANIMALS

Transportation of Deer

Section 208:15-i

    208:15-i Emergency Closure; Limitation on Availability. – The executive director may, by administrative rule adopted pursuant to RSA 541-A, limit the availability of online registration on days designated for biological sample collection and suspend online registration in the event of a wildlife or public health emergency, with the consent of the commission.

Source. 2022, 135:3, eff. Aug. 6, 2022.
