TITLE XXXVI
PAWNBROKERS AND MONEYLENDERS

CHAPTER 399-G
LICENSING OF MONEY TRANSMITTERS

Section 399-G:37

    399-G:37 Application; Construction of Chapter. – In applying and construing this chapter, consideration shall be given to the need to promote uniformity of the law with respect to its subject matter among states that enact similar regulation.

Source. 2024, 368:1, eff. Oct. 22, 2024.
