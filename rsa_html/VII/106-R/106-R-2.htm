TITLE VII
SHERIFFS, CONSTABLES, AND POLICE OFFICERS

CHAPTER 106-R
ANTI-SANCTUARY ACT

Section 106-R:2

    106-R:2 Prohibition of Sanctuary Policies. – No state government entity, local government entity, or law enforcement agency shall knowingly enact, issue, adopt, promulgate, enforce, permit, endorse, maintain, or have in effect any sanctuary policy.

Source. 2025, 39:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2026.
