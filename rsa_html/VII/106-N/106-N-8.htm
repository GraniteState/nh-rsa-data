TITLE VII
SHERIFFS, CONSTABLES, AND POLICE OFFICERS

CHAPTER 106-N
RAILROAD POLICE

Section 106-N:8

    106-N:8 Compensation. – The railroad to which each railroad police officer is assigned shall be responsible for employment and retirement compensation and for the financial cost of training of railroad police officers.

Source. 1979, 331:1, eff. Aug. 21, 1979. RSA 381:8. 2024, 33:10, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 106-N:8.
