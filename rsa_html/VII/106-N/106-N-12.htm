TITLE VII
SHERIFFS, CONSTABLES, AND POLICE OFFICERS

CHAPTER 106-N
RAILROAD POLICE

Section 106-N:12

    106-N:12 Impersonating an Officer. – Any person who impersonates a railroad police officer shall be guilty of a class B felony.

Source. 1979, 331:1. RSA 381:12. 2008, 225:2, eff. Jan. 1, 2009. 2024, 33:10, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 106-N:12.
