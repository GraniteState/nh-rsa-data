TITLE VII
SHERIFFS, CONSTABLES, AND POLICE OFFICERS

CHAPTER 106-N
RAILROAD POLICE

Section 106-N:5

    106-N:5 Powers. – Any police officer appointed in accordance with this chapter shall have the authority in all cases in which the rights of the appointing railroad are involved to exercise within this state all the powers of a special police officer, including the powers of arrest and the carrying of firearms, for the reasonable purpose of his office.

Source. 1979, 331:1, eff. Aug. 21, 1979. RSA 381:5. 2024, 33:10, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 106-N:5.
