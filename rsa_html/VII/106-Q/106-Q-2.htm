TITLE VII
SHERIFFS, CONSTABLES, AND POLICE OFFICERS

CHAPTER 106-Q
ANTI-SANCTUARY CITY ACT

Section 106-Q:2

    106-Q:2 Prohibition of Sanctuary Policies. – Except as otherwise expressly prohibited by state or federal law, local governmental entities may not prohibit or impede any state or federal law enforcement agency from complying with title 8 of the United States Code.

Source. 2025, 38:2, eff. Jan. 1, 2026.
