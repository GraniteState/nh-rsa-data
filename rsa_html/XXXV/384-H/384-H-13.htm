TITLE XXXV
BANKS AND BANKING; LOAN ASSOCIATIONS; CREDIT UNIONS

CHAPTER 384-H
UNIFORM SPECIAL DEPOSITS ACT

Section 384-H:13

    384-H:13 Term and Termination. –
I. Unless otherwise provided in the account agreement, a special deposit shall terminate 5 years after the date the special deposit was first funded.
II. Unless otherwise provided in the account agreement, if the bank cannot identify or locate a beneficiary entitled to payment when the special deposit is terminated, and a balance remains in the special deposit, the bank shall pay the balance to the depositor or depositors as a beneficiary or beneficiaries.
III. A bank that pays the remaining balance as provided in this chapter has no further obligation with respect to the special deposit.

Source. 2026, 228:1, eff. Aug. 31, 2026.
