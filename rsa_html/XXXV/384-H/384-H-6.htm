TITLE XXXV
BANKS AND BANKING; LOAN ASSOCIATIONS; CREDIT UNIONS

CHAPTER 384-H
UNIFORM SPECIAL DEPOSITS ACT

Section 384-H:6

    384-H:6 Permissible Purpose. –
I. A special deposit shall serve at least one permissible purpose stated in the account agreement from the time the special deposit is created in the account agreement until termination of the special deposit.
II. If, before termination of the special deposit, the bank or a court determines the special deposit no longer satisfies paragraph I, RSA 384-H:8 through RSA 384-H:11 shall cease to apply to any funds deposited in the special deposit after the special deposit ceases to satisfy paragraph I.
III. If, before termination of a special deposit, the bank determines the special deposit no longer satisfies paragraph I, the bank may take action it believes is necessary under the circumstances, including terminating the special deposit.

Source. 2026, 228:1, eff. Aug. 31, 2026.
