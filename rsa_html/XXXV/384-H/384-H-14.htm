TITLE XXXV
BANKS AND BANKING; LOAN ASSOCIATIONS; CREDIT UNIONS

CHAPTER 384-H
UNIFORM SPECIAL DEPOSITS ACT

Section 384-H:14

    384-H:14 Principles of Law and Equity. – The Uniform Commercial Code, RSA 382-A:9-101 to 382-A:9-628, and law governing consumer protection, law governing deposits generally, law related to escheat and abandoned or unclaimed property, and the principles of law and equity, including law related to capacity to contract, principal and agent, estoppel, fraud, misrepresentation, duress, coercion, mistake, and bankruptcy, may supplement this chapter except to the extent inconsistent with this chapter.

Source. 2026, 228:1, eff. Aug. 31, 2026.
