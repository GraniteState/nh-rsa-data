TITLE XXXV
BANKS AND BANKING; LOAN ASSOCIATIONS; CREDIT UNIONS

CHAPTER 384-H
UNIFORM SPECIAL DEPOSITS ACT

Section 384-H:15

    384-H:15 Uniformity of Application and Construction. – In applying and construing this uniform act, a court shall consider the promotion of uniformity of the law among jurisdictions that enact it.

Source. 2026, 228:1, eff. Aug. 31, 2026.
