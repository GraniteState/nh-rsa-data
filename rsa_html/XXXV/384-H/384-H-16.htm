TITLE XXXV
BANKS AND BANKING; LOAN ASSOCIATIONS; CREDIT UNIONS

CHAPTER 384-H
UNIFORM SPECIAL DEPOSITS ACT

Section 384-H:16

    384-H:16 Transitional Provision. –
This chapter shall apply to:
I. A special deposit made under an account agreement executed on or after the effective date of this chapter; and
II. A deposit made under an agreement executed before the effective date of this chapter, if:
(a) All parties entitled to amend the agreement agree to make the deposit a special deposit governed by this chapter; and
(b) The special deposit referenced in the amended agreement satisfies RSA 384-H:5.

Source. 2026, 228:1, eff. Aug. 31, 2026.
