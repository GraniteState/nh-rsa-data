TITLE XXXV
BANKS AND BANKING; LOAN ASSOCIATIONS; CREDIT UNIONS

CHAPTER 384-H
UNIFORM SPECIAL DEPOSITS ACT

Section 384-H:8

    384-H:8 Property Interest of Depositor or Beneficiary. –
I. Neither a depositor nor a beneficiary shall have a property interest in a special deposit.
II. Any property interest with respect to a special deposit shall be only in the right to receive payment if the bank is obligated to pay a beneficiary and not in the special deposit itself. Any property interest under this paragraph is determined under other law.

Source. 2026, 228:1, eff. Aug. 31, 2026.
