TITLE XV
EDUCATION

CHAPTER 189
SCHOOL BOARDS, SUPERINTENDENTS, TEACHERS, AND TRUANT OFFICERS; SCHOOL CENSUS

Reports

Section 189:29-c


[RSA 189:29-c effective October 8, 2026.]
    189:29-c Report on District Activity. –
Every 3 years following the effective date of this section, the school board of every school district shall:
I. Review the existing contracts, debts, funding, policies, and salaries of the school district; and
II. Provide a written report to the school district including findings and recommendations relative to such district activity.

Source. 2026, 303:8, eff. Oct. 8, 2026.
