TITLE XV
EDUCATION

CHAPTER 194-F
EDUCATION FREEDOM ACCOUNTS

Section 194-F:8

    194-F:8 Responsibilities of Public Schools and School Districts. – A public school, or school district, that previously enrolled an EFA student shall provide a private school that is also an education service provider and that has enrolled an EFA student with a complete copy of the ESA student's school records, in a timely manner, while complying with 20 U.S.C. section 1232g, the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act of 1974.

Source. 2021, 91:431, eff. Aug. 24, 2021.
