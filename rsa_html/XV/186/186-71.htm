TITLE XV
EDUCATION

CHAPTER 186
THE STATE SCHOOL ORGANIZATION

Prohibition on Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion in Public Schools

Section 186:71

    186:71 Definitions. –
In this subdivision:
I. "Diversity, equity, and inclusion" or "DEI" shall mean any program, policy, training, or initiative that classifies individuals based on a characteristic identified under RSA 354-A:1 for the purpose of achieving demographic outcomes, rather than treating individuals equally under the law.
II. "Public school" means any school, academic institution, or institution of higher education in this state supported by public funds.

Source. 2025, 141:322, eff. July 1, 2025.
