TITLE XV
EDUCATION

CHAPTER 195
COOPERATIVE SCHOOL DISTRICTS

Section 195:10-a


[RSA 195:10-a effective October 8, 2026.]
    195:10-a School Closures. –
All cooperative school districts shall comply with the requirements of RSA 194:3-f and RSA 194:3-g relative to the closure of elementary and high schools.
I. For the purposes of this section:
(a) Elementary schools are the same as defined in RSA 189:25.
(b) High schools are the same as defined in RSA 194:23.
II. It shall be prohibited for any cooperative school district articles of agreement to circumvent the requirements of RSA 194:3-f and RSA 194:3-g.

Source. 2026, 303:5, eff. Oct. 8, 2026.
