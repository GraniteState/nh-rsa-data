TITLE XXIII
LABOR

CHAPTER 277
SAFETY AND HEALTH OF EMPLOYEES

Inspections and Orders by Commissioner

Section 277:15-a

    277:15-a Inspections. – In the case of a workplace incident resulting in a serious injury or death involving the state, a state agency, a county, a municipality or municipal agency, a school district, or other public corporation or political subdivision, the commissioner of labor, or his or her designee, shall inspect such place of employment.

Source. 2019, 29:2, eff. July 14, 2019.
