TITLE XXIII
LABOR

CHAPTER 275-C
GOVERNOR'S COMMISSION ON DISABILITY

Telecommunications Equipment Assistance Program

Section 275-C:24

    275-C:24 Funding. – The telecommunications equipment assistance program shall be funded in an amount not to exceed $96,000 annually from the telecommunications relay service trust fund established by the public utilities commission.

Source. 2024, 33:15, eff. July 1, 2024.
