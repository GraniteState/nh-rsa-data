TITLE XXIII
LABOR

CHAPTER 282-B
PURCHASING POOL FOR FAMILY AND MEDICAL LEAVE INSURANCE

Section 282-B:9

    282-B:9 Implementation. – The individual pool shall be operational and available for use by individuals on a timetable that is sufficient to ensure that FMLI coverage shall be available for purchase by January 1, 2023.

Source. 2021, 91:466, eff. July 1, 2021.
