TITLE XXIII
LABOR

CHAPTER 282-B
PURCHASING POOL FOR FAMILY AND MEDICAL LEAVE INSURANCE

Section 282-B:8

    282-B:8 Appropriation and Funding Transfer. – The state treasurer shall transfer funds from the general fund to the department of employment security for payment of the administrative and implementation costs associated with this chapter.

Source. 2021, 91:466, eff. July 1, 2021.
