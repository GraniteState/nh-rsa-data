TITLE LI
COURTS

CHAPTER 490-I
VETERANS TRACK

Section 490-I:3

    490-I:3 Eligibility. – A veteran may be eligible for veterans court if he or she has experienced military sexual trauma or has a service-related mental condition, service-related traumatic brain injury, service-related substance use disorder, or service-related psychological disorder.

Source. 2024, 371:1, eff. July 1, 2025.
