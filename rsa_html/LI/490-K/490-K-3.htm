TITLE LI
COURTS

CHAPTER 490-K
JUDICIAL TRAINING

Section 490-K:3

    490-K:3 Judicial Continuing Education Requirements. – Any person who holds the position of judge, court administrator, clerk, or director of the administrative office of the court in this state, shall be required to complete continuing education as the chief justice of the supreme court directs.

Source. 2024, 317:11, eff. July 1, 2025. 2025, 3:1, eff. Sept. 21, 2025.
