TITLE XXVII
CORPORATIONS, ASSOCIATIONS, AND PROPRIETORS OF COMMON LANDS

CHAPTER 301-B
DECENTRALIZED AUTONOMOUS ORGANIZATIONS

Section 301-B:30

    301-B:30 Reserved Power of State of New Hampshire to Alter or Repeal Act. – All provisions of this act may be altered from time to time or repealed, and all rights of New Hampshire DAOs, participants, administrators, and legal representatives are subject to this reservation.

Source. 2024, 263:1, eff. July 1, 2025.
