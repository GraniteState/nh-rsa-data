TITLE XXVII
CORPORATIONS, ASSOCIATIONS, AND PROPRIETORS OF COMMON LANDS

CHAPTER 301-B
DECENTRALIZED AUTONOMOUS ORGANIZATIONS

Section 301-B:1

    301-B:1 Short Title. – This act may be cited as the "New Hampshire Decentralized Autonomous Organization Act" (the "Act").

Source. 2024, 263:1, eff. July 1, 2025.
