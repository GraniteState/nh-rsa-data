TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 250
MAINTENANCE OF PARKS BY RAILROADS AND BUS LINES

Section 250:1

    250:1 Authority. – With the consent of the department of transportation, and upon such terms as it may approve, railroads and bus lines may hold or acquire, by lease or purchase, suitable lands, and convert and maintain the same, as a part of their system, into parks and pleasure grounds for the accommodation and recreation of the public.

Source. 1897, 51:1. PL 257:1. RL 303:1. 1951, 203:62 par. 1. RSA 382:1. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(8). 2024, 33:13, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 250:1.
