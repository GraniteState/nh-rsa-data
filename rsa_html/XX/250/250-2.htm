TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 250
MAINTENANCE OF PARKS BY RAILROADS AND BUS LINES

Section 250:2

    250:2 Structures. – Such lands may be properly enclosed and suitable buildings and structures may be erected and maintained thereon for the convenience, entertainment and comfort of the public.

Source. 1897, 51:1. PL 257:2. RL 303:2. 1951, 203:62 par. 2, eff. Sept. 1, 1951. RSA 382:2. 2024, 33:13, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 250:2.
