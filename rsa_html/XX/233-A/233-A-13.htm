TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 233-A
ACCESS TO PUBLIC WATERS

Public Boat Access Program

Section 233-A:13

    233-A:13 Statewide Public Boat Access Fund Established. – There is hereby established a nonlapsing statewide public boat access fund. The $5 boat decal fee collected pursuant to RSA 270-E:5-a, II(c), and any other public access funds donated to the state shall be placed in this fund. Funds received under this section are continually appropriated to the fish and game department for the sole purposes of the statewide public boat access program established under this chapter, except for sufficient funds which are hereby appropriated to the department of natural and cultural resources for payment of principal and interest on bonds and notes for the Mount Sunapee state park beach boat ramp project.

Source. 1992, 265:2, eff. Jan. 1, 1993. 2019, 346:180, eff. July 1, 2019. 2025, 84:1, eff. Aug. 9, 2025.
