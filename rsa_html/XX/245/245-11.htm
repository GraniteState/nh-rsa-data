TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 245
LAYING OUT RAILROADS

Section 245:11

    245:11 Abeyance of Appraisal. – No appraisal of damages to landowners shall be made, after a petition for a change of location has been presented to the department of transportation, until such question has been decided.

Source. GS 146:13. GL 160:13. PS 158:10. PL 248:11. RL 298:11. 1951, 203:1. RSA 372:11. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(5). 2024, 33:3, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 245:11.
