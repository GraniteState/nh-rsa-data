TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 245
LAYING OUT RAILROADS

Section 245:24

    245:24 Remedies Where Land Is Taken for Yards. – In the cases provided for in RSA 245:23, the parties shall have like remedies for a change of location, including a modification of the right taken, and for an appraisal of damages, as in the case of taking land for a railroad, with the same right of appeal and the same procedure thereon.

Source. 1883, 84:2. PS 158:29. PL 248:24. RL 298:24. RSA 372:24. 2024, 33:3, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 245:24.
