TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 245
LAYING OUT RAILROADS

Section 245:1

    245:1 Agents' Duties. – Railroads shall be surveyed, and laid out, in the first instance, by their agents.

Source. GS 146:4. GL 160:4. PS 158:1. PL 248:1. RL 298:1. 1951, 203:56, eff. Sept. 1, 1951. RSA 372:1. 2024, 33:3, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 245:1.
