TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 245
LAYING OUT RAILROADS

Section 245:16

    245:16 Payment to State. – If the landowner or his residence is unknown, or if he is a minor and has no guardian, or is not resident in the state, the damages awarded shall be paid to the state treasurer for the landowner's use, before the land can be rightfully entered upon.

Source. CS 150:26, 27. GS 146:21. GL 160:21. PS 158:19. PL 248:16. RL 298:16. RSA 373:16. 2024, 33:3, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 245:16.
