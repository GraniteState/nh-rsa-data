TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 245
LAYING OUT RAILROADS

Section 245:19

    245:19 Action. – No action shall be brought for damages before entry upon the land; and, if the location of the road shall be changed before the land is entered upon for the purpose of building the road, no damages shall be paid.

Source. 1845, 227:4. 1847, 485:3. CS 150:14, 16, 17. GS 146:23. GL 160:23. PS 158:22. PL 248:19. RL 298:19. RSA 372:19. 2024, 33:3, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 245:19.
