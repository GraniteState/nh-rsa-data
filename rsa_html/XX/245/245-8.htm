TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 245
LAYING OUT RAILROADS

Section 245:8

    245:8 Conveyances. – The corporation, either before or after such location, may obtain deeds, or bonds for deeds, of any lands which it deems necessary for its road, or of the right of way over the same.

Source. GS 146:9. GL 160:9. PS 158:8. PL 248:8. RL 298:8. RSA 372:8. 2024, 33:3, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 245:8.
