TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 245
LAYING OUT RAILROADS

Section 245:20

    245:20 Change After Construction. – The location of a railroad already built may be changed by the department of transportation upon petition of the proprietors thereof, after notice to all persons interested, a hearing and an award of damages to persons injured by the change.

Source. 1847, 485:1. CS 150:12. GS 146:18. GL 160:18. PS 158:23. PL 248:20. RL 298:20. 1951, 203:1. RSA 372:20. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(5). 2024, 33:3, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 245:20.
