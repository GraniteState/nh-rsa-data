TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 245
LAYING OUT RAILROADS

Section 245:10

    245:10 By Landowner. – The owner of any interest in any land over which the road is located, at any time after the return of the location is made as above provided, may apply in like manner by petition to the department of transportation to appraise the damages occasioned to him by the railroad.

Source. 1844, 128:5. CS 150:10. GS 146:10. GL 160:10. PS 158:9. 1913, 49:1. PL 248:10. RL 298:10. 1951, 203:1. RSA 372:10. 1985, 402:6, I (e)(5). 2024, 33:3, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 245:10.
