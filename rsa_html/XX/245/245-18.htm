TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 245
LAYING OUT RAILROADS

Section 245:18

    245:18 Costs. – If the result of an appeal is to change the award of damages in favor of the appellant, the appellant shall recover costs; otherwise he shall pay costs.

Source. 1844, 128:5. CS 150:11. GS 146:17. GL 160:17. PS 158:21. PL 248:18. RL 298:18. RSA 372:18. 2024, 33:3, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 245:18.
