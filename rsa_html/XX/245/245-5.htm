TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 245
LAYING OUT RAILROADS

Section 245:5

    245:5 Times of Location. – Persons authorized to construct and maintain a railroad as provided in RSA 245:2 may locate and lay out the road in its entire course at one time, or in parts at different and successive times, as they shall deem conducive to the interests of all concerned.

Source. CS 150:21. GS 146:4. GL 160:4. PS 158:5. PL 248:5. RL 298:5. RSA 372:5. 2024, 33:3, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 245:5.
