TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 244
RAILROAD TRUSTEES AND HOLDING COMPANIES

Section 244:2

    244:2 Failure to Call Meeting. – If the trustees, on application of such creditors to the amount of 1/3 of the whole sum secured, do not, within 14 days, call such meeting, 5 or more creditors holding the like amount of claims may call it in the same manner.

Source. 1858, 2100:1. GS 151:2. GL 165:2. PS 161:2. PL 252:2. RL 302:2. RSA 368:2. 2024, 33:2, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 244:2.
