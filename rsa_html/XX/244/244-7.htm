TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 244
RAILROAD TRUSTEES AND HOLDING COMPANIES

Section 244:7

    244:7 Liability of Company. – In case of such damage, the company assigning or mortgaging the railroad shall be liable; and the assets in the hands of the trustees shall be held for such damage as part of the expenses of the trust, in preference to the claims of the general creditors of the company.

Source. 1858, 2100:5. GS 151:7. GL 165:7. PS 161:7. PL 252:7. RL 302:7. RSA 368:7. 2024, 33:2, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 244:7.
