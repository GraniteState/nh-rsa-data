TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

Regulation of Railroads

Section 243:53

    243:53 Recording Order. – If the petition shall be granted, the railroad corporation shall file in the office of the secretary of state a copy of the petition and of the order of the commissioner.

Source. 1985, 402:19. RSA 367:53. 2024, 33:1, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 243:53.
