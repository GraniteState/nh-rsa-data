TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

Union of Two or More Railroad Corporations

Section 243:24

    243:24 Right to Vote. – Until stock of the new corporation has been issued to assenting stockholders, they shall have the right to vote in meetings of members of the new corporation as if stock had been issued to them according to the agreement of union and they were the holders thereof.

Source. PS 156:25. PL 246:22. RL 296:22. RSA 367:24. 2024, 33:1, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 243:24.
