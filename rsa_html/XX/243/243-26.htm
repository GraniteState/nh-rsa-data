TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

Union of Two or More Railroad Corporations

Section 243:26

    243:26 Limit of Dividends. – The new corporation shall not divide to its stockholders in dividends a greater sum than the aggregate sum which the uniting corporations together were authorized to divide to their stockholders.

Source. 1883, 100:24. PS 156:27. PL 246:24. RL 296:24. RSA 367:26. 2024, 33:1, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 243:26.
