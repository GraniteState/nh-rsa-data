TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

Dissenting Stockholders

Section 243:40

    243:40 Hiring Money. – A corporation may borrow money to settle with its dissenting stockholders; provided, that its indebtedness is not thereby increased beyond the limit authorized by law.

Source. PS 156:40. PL 246:38. RL 296:38. RSA 367:40. 2024, 33:1, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 243:40.
