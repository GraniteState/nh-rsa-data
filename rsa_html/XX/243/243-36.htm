TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

Dissenting Stockholders

Section 243:36

    243:36 Appeal. – Any stockholder who is aggrieved by the award may appeal therefrom by filing, in the office of the clerk of the superior court for the county of Merrimack, notice of his appeal, within 30 days after the award is filed.

Source. 1889, 5:1. PS 156:35. PL 246:34. RL 296:34. RSA 367:36. 2024, 33:1, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 243:36.
