TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

Formation of a Railroad Corporation

Section 243:13

    243:13 Hearing; Report. – The board shall hear all parties who are interested and desire to be heard and shall make report of their doings and findings to the regular session of the court held next after their last hearing.

Source. PS 156:12. PL 246:12. RL 296:12. RSA 367:13. 2024, 33:1, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 243:13.
