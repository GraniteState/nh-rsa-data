TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

General Provisions

Section 243:43

    243:43 Place of Business. – The principal place of business of every corporation formed under the provisions of this chapter, and the offices of the superintendent and other officers who have the general management of the business of the corporation, shall be within this state.

Source. 1883, 100:17. PS 156:39. PL 246:41. RL 296:41. RSA 367:43. 2024, 33:1, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 243:43.
