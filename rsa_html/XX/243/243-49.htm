TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

Application of Chapter

Section 243:49

    243:49 Rights of State. – The rights of the state as to any corporation, its franchises or property shall not be impaired by any contract of lease or union made by it.

Source. PS 156:45. PL 246:44. RL 296:44. RSA 367:49. 2024, 33:1, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 243:49.
