TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

Application of Chapter

Section 243:47

    243:47 Corporate Rights. – Nothing in this chapter shall impair the right which any railroad corporation has to unite with another corporation, or to become a party to a contract for the lease of a railroad.

Source. 1889, 5:16. PS 156:43. PL 246:42. RL 296:42. RSA 367:47. 2024, 33:1, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 243:47.
