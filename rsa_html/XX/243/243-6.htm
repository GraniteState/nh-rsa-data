TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

Formation of a Railroad Corporation

Section 243:6

    243:6 Fees. – The fee for recording articles of agreement in the office of the secretary of state as provided in RSA 243:5 shall be at the rate of $1 per page.

Source. 1949, 265:8, eff. June 22, 1949. RSA 367:6. 2024, 33:1, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 243:6.
