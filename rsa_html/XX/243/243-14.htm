TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

Formation of a Railroad Corporation

Section 243:14

    243:14 Decision. – The court shall thereupon, after hearing such parties as desire to be heard, decide the questions raised by the petition, and cause a record of their decision to be made.

Source. PS 156:13. PL 246:13. RL 296:13. RSA 367:14. 2024, 33:1, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 243:14.
