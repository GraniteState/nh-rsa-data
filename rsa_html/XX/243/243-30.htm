TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

Dissenting Stockholders

Section 243:30

    243:30 Additional Parties. – Any nonassenting stockholders may appear and be heard upon the questions before the court affecting their rights.

Source. 1889, 5:1. PS 156:30. PL 246:28. RL 296:28. RSA 367:30. 2024, 33:1, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 243:30.
