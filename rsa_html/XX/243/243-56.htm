TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

Regulation of Railroads

Section 243:56

    243:56 Accidents. – The commissioner shall require notification and reports of all accidents happening on all railroads in the state and shall investigate the causes of such accidents.

Source. 1985, 402:19. RSA 367:56. 1989, 9:1. 1997, 290:1, eff. Aug. 19, 1997. 2024, 33:1, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 243:56.
