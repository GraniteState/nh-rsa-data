TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

Formation of a Railroad Corporation

Section 243:18

    243:18 Powers of Railroad Corporations Generally. – Railroads have the general powers granted to other corporations, and the special powers granted to them by their charters and the general laws of the state.

Source. GS 144:1. GL 158:1. PS 157:6. PL 247:6. RL 297:6. 1951, 203:50, eff. Sept. 1, 1951. RSA 367:18. 2024, 33:1, July 1, 2024. RSA 243:18.
