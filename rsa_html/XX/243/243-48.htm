TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

Application of Chapter

Section 243:48

    243:48 Foreign Corporation. – Foreign railroad corporations operating roads within this state shall have the same rights for the purposes of operating, leasing or uniting with other roads as if created by the laws of this state.

Source. 1883, 100:18. PS 156:44. PL 246:43. RL 296:43. RSA 367:48. 2024, 33:1, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 243:48.
