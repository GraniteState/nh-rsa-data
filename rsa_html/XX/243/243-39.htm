TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

Dissenting Stockholders

Section 243:39

    243:39 Judgment; Costs. – If upon such trial the amount of the award is increased, the stockholder shall have judgment and execution against the appellee therefor, with interest and costs; if it is decreased, the appellee shall have judgment and execution for such decrease.

Source. 1889, 5:1. PS 156:37. PL 246:37. RL 296:37. RSA 367:39. 2024, 33:1, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 243:39.
