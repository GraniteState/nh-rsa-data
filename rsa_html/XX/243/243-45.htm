TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

General Provisions

Section 243:45

    243:45 Adverse Possession. – No title to any real estate or to any interest therein shall be acquired by or against a railroad by adverse possession, however exclusive or long continued.

Source. 1862, 2625:1. 1865, 4085:1. GS 146:25. GL 160:25. PS 157:15. PL 247:13. RL 298:13. 1951, 203:54, eff. Sept. 1, 1951. RSA 367:45. 2024, 33:1, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 243:45.
