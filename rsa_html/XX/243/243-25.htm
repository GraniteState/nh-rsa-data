TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 243
FORMATION AND UNION OF RAILROAD CORPORATIONS; LEASES AND EXTENSIONS

Union of Two or More Railroad Corporations

Section 243:25

    243:25 Limit of Stock. – The capital stock of the new corporation shall not exceed the sum of the capital stocks of the uniting corporations actually issued and paid for at par at the time of the union.

Source. 1883, 100:23. PS 156:26. PL 246:23. RL 296:23. RSA 367:25. 2024, 33:1, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 243:25.
