TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 249
RAILROADS; FIRE DAMAGE AND PREVENTION

Prevention, Etc., of Fires

Section 249:8

    249:8 Expenses. – All just and proper expenses incurred in extinguishing forest or brush fires caused by a railroad shall be paid by such railroad; but the fact that such payment has been made shall not be admissible as evidence that such fire was so caused.

Source. 1913, 155:2. PL 250:8. RL 300:8. 1951, 203:59 par. 8, eff. Sept. 1, 1951. RSA 380:8. 2024, 33:9, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 249:8.
