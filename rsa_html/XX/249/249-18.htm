TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 249
RAILROADS; FIRE DAMAGE AND PREVENTION

Entry on Land

Section 249:18

    249:18 Reports. – The department of natural and cultural resources or its authorized agents shall have the right to enter upon railroad or other property to ascertain facts, and from time to time shall report the same to the department of transportation.

Source. 1913, 155:5. PL 250:18. RL 300:18. 1951, 203:59 par. 18. RSA 380:18. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(8). 2017, 156:14, I, eff. July 1, 2017. 2024, 33:9, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 249:18.
