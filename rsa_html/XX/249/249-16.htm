TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 249
RAILROADS; FIRE DAMAGE AND PREVENTION

Entry on Land

Section 249:16

    249:16 Hearing; Order. – After a hearing, the department of transportation may sustain the objection or permit the clearing to be done, and may prescribe the extent and methods of such clearing.

Source. 1913, 155:4. PL 250:16. RL 300:16. 1951, 203:59 par. 16. RSA 380:16. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(8). 2024, 33:9, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 249:16.
