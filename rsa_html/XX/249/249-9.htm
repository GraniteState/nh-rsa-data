TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 249
RAILROADS; FIRE DAMAGE AND PREVENTION

Prevention, Etc., of Fires

Section 249:9

    249:9 Instruction to Employees. – Railroads shall promulgate among their employees instructions for the prevention and extinguishment of fires along the right of way.

Source. 1913, 155:3. PL 250:9. RL 300:9. 1951, 203:59 par. 9, eff. Sept. 1, 1951. RSA 380:9. 2024, 33:9, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 249:9.
