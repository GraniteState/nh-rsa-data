TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 249
RAILROADS; FIRE DAMAGE AND PREVENTION

Entry on Land

Section 249:15

    249:15 Objection. – Upon the filing of such an objection, the department of transportation shall notify the owner of the time and place when he may appear to show cause why such clearing should not be done.

Source. 1913, 155:4. PL 250:15. RL 300:15. 1951, 203:59 par. 15. RSA 380:15. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(8). 2024, 33:9, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 249:15.
