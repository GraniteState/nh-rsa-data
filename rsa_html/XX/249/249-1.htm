TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 249
RAILROADS; FIRE DAMAGE AND PREVENTION

Liability of Railroads for Fire Damage

Section 249:1

    249:1 Railroad's Liability. – Every railroad shall be liable for all damages to any person or property by fire or steam from any locomotive or other engine upon their road.

Source. RS 142:8. CS 150:59. GS 148:8. GL 162:8. PS 159:29. PL 250:1. RL 300:1. 1951, 203:59 par. 1, eff. Sept. 1, 1951. RSA 380:1. 2024, 33:9, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 249:1.
