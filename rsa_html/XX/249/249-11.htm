TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 249
RAILROADS; FIRE DAMAGE AND PREVENTION

Prevention, Etc., of Fires

Section 249:11

    249:11 Neglect. – The fact that a section of the right of way was not patrolled shall not be evidence of negligence to debar such railroad from insurance on property as provided in RSA 249:3.

Source. 1913, 155:3. PL 250:11. RL 300:11. 1951, 203:59 par. 11, eff. Sept. 1, 1951. RSA 380:11. 2024, 33:9, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 249:11.
