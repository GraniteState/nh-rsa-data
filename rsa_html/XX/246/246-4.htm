TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 246
CROSSINGS, STATIONS, FENCES, CATTLE GUARDS, BRIDGES, BRIDGE GUARDS

Grade Crossings

Section 246:4

    246:4 Consent of Department of Transportation. – No railroad hereafter constructed shall cross another railroad, a highway or other way, at grade, unless the consent in writing of the department of transportation is first obtained.

Source. 1885, 98:5, 6. 1889, 65:1. PS 159:2. PL 249:2. RL 299:5. 1951, 203:58 par. 4. RSA 373:4. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(5). 2024, 33:4, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 246:4.
