TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 246
CROSSINGS, STATIONS, FENCES, CATTLE GUARDS, BRIDGES, BRIDGE GUARDS

Stopping Places and Depots

Section 246:28

    246:28 Order to Establish. – The department of transportation, upon petition of the selectmen or of 20 or more legal voters of a town, after notice and hearing, may order a railroad to establish such stopping places or depots in the town as it finds that the public good requires, within a time by it limited, and to stop trains at such stopping places or depots.

Source. 1850, 953:6. CS 150:61. GS 147:14. GL 161:14. PS 159:21. PL 249:37. RL 299:45. 1951, 203:58 par. 28. RSA 373:28. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(5). 2024, 33:4, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 246:28.
