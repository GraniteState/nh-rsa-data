TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 246
CROSSINGS, STATIONS, FENCES, CATTLE GUARDS, BRIDGES, BRIDGE GUARDS

Crossing Protection

Section 246:20

    246:20 Procedure. – Such petition shall set forth the title of the land involved, a description of the land, or of the rights and easements, and the purpose for which required, and proceedings shall thereafter be had as provided in RSA 245.

Source. 1951, 203:58 par. 20, eff. Sept. 1, 1951. RSA 373:20. 2024, 33:4, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 246:20.
