TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 246
CROSSINGS, STATIONS, FENCES, CATTLE GUARDS, BRIDGES, BRIDGE GUARDS

Closing of Highway Crossings

Section 246:26

    246:26 Appeal. – Any party aggrieved by the assessment of the department of transportation may, within 60 days after the report of the department of transportation thereon and not afterwards, appeal to the superior court in the county where such crossing may be situated, and the court shall assess the same by jury and award costs to the prevailing party.

Source. 1935, 110:5. RL 299:43. 1951, 203:58 par. 26. RSA 373:26. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(5). 2024, 33:4, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 246:26.
