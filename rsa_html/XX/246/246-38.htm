TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 246
CROSSINGS, STATIONS, FENCES, CATTLE GUARDS, BRIDGES, BRIDGE GUARDS

Bridges and Bridge Guards

Section 246:38

    246:38 Neglect to Comply. – Neglect by any railroad to comply with the orders of the department of transportation, within a reasonable time to be specified in such orders, shall be punished by a forfeiture of $50 a day, to be collected by the department of transportation, in the name and for the use of the state, in an action of debt.

Source. 1893, 39:3. PL 249:49. RL 299:57. 1951, 203:58 par. 38. RSA 373:38. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(5). 2024, 33:4, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 246:38.
