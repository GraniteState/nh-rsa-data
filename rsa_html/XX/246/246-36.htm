TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 246
CROSSINGS, STATIONS, FENCES, CATTLE GUARDS, BRIDGES, BRIDGE GUARDS

Bridges and Bridge Guards

Section 246:36

    246:36 Notice. – Proceedings under this subdivision shall only be had after due notice to the railroad, the town or city and the landowners.

Source. 1893, 39:1. PL 249:47. RL 299:55. 1951, 203:58 par. 36, eff. Sept. 1, 1951. RSA 373:36. 2024, 33:4, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 246:36.
