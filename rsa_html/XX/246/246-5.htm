TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 246
CROSSINGS, STATIONS, FENCES, CATTLE GUARDS, BRIDGES, BRIDGE GUARDS

Grade Crossings

Section 246:5

    246:5 Neglect to Obtain. – If a railroad shall neglect to comply with the requirements of RSA 246:4, the department of transportation may subsequently approve the crossing made by the railroad, or may order it to make such changes therein as the public good requires. If the railroad shall fail to comply with such order, it shall be guilty of a felony.

Source. PS 159:3. PL 249:3. RL 299:6. 1951, 203:58 par. 5. RSA 373:5. 1973, 529:88. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(5). 2024, 33:4, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 246:5.
