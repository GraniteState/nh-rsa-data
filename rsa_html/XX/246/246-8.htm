TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 246
CROSSINGS, STATIONS, FENCES, CATTLE GUARDS, BRIDGES, BRIDGE GUARDS

Grade Crossings

Section 246:8

    246:8 Appeal. – Either party shall have the right to appeal from the decision of the commissioner to the superior court.

Source. 1895, 91:1. PL 249:9. RL 299:9. 1951, 203:58 par. 8. RSA 373:8. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(5). 2024, 33:4, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 246:8.
