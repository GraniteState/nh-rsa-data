TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 246
CROSSINGS, STATIONS, FENCES, CATTLE GUARDS, BRIDGES, BRIDGE GUARDS

Cattle Guards, Etc., for Accommodation of Individuals

Section 246:34

    246:34 Failure to Construct. – If the railroad does not construct such cattle guards, passes and crossings within the times limited by the department of transportation, and does not pay the costs adjudged to be paid by it, upon request, it shall be guilty of a violation for each month's neglect.

Source. 1853, 1416:1. GS 147:17. GL 161:17. 1889, 54:1. PS 159:20. PL 249:43. RL 299:51. 1951, 203:58 par. 34. RSA 373:34. 1973, 531:111. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(5). 2024, 33:4, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 246:34.
