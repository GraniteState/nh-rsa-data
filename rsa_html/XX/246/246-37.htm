TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 246
CROSSINGS, STATIONS, FENCES, CATTLE GUARDS, BRIDGES, BRIDGE GUARDS

Bridges and Bridge Guards

Section 246:37

    246:37 Orders. – All orders and findings of the department of transportation shall be filed with the clerk of the town or city in which such bridge is located, and served upon the railroad.

Source. 1893, 39:1. PL 249:48. RL 299:56. 1951, 203:58 par. 37. RSA 373:37. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(5). 2024, 33:4, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 246:37.
