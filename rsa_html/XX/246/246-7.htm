TITLE XX
TRANSPORTATION

CHAPTER 246
CROSSINGS, STATIONS, FENCES, CATTLE GUARDS, BRIDGES, BRIDGE GUARDS

Grade Crossings

Section 246:7

    246:7 Expense. – In the event that a new railroad or highway is located or laid across an existing railroad or highway, the cost of construction and maintenance shall be apportioned between the parties in interest in accordance with the principles set forth in RSA 246:3.

Source. 1895, 91:1. PL 249:5. RL 299:8. 1951, 203:58 par. 7, eff. Sept. 1, 1951. RSA 373:7. 2024, 33:4, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 246:7.
