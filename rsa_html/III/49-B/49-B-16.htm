TITLE III
TOWNS, CITIES, VILLAGE DISTRICTS, AND UNINCORPORATED PLACES

CHAPTER 49-B
HOME RULE-MUNICIPAL CHARTERS

Derry Cooperative School District Charter

Section 49-B:16

    49-B:16 Derry Cooperative School District Charter Amendment. – The Derry town council may incorporate the Derry Cooperative School District as a department of the town through a charter amendment to give the town council control over the form and procedures for preparation and adoption of the school department budget.

Source. 2026, 165:1, eff. June 19, 2026.
