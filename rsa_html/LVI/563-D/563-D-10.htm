TITLE LVI
PROBATE COURTS AND DECEDENTS' ESTATES

CHAPTER 563-D
UNIFORM REAL PROPERTY TRANSFER ON DEATH ACT

Section 563-D:10

    563-D:10 Notice, Delivery, Acceptance, Consideration Not Required. –
A transfer on death deed is effective without:
I. Notice or delivery to or acceptance by the designated beneficiary during the transferor's life; or
II. Consideration.

Source. 2024, 1:1, eff. July 1, 2024.
