TITLE LVI
PROBATE COURTS AND DECEDENTS' ESTATES

CHAPTER 564-F
NEW HAMPSHIRE FOUNDATION ACT

ARTICLE 10
Directors

Section 564-F:10-1010

    564-F:10-1010 Voting. –
(a) Unless the governing documents provides otherwise, each director shall have one vote on each matter on which the directors may vote.
(b) Unless the governing documents provide otherwise, each matter on which the directors may vote shall be decided by vote of a majority of the directors.

Source. 2017, 257:47, eff. Oct. 1, 2017.
