TITLE LVI
PROBATE COURTS AND DECEDENTS' ESTATES

CHAPTER 564-F
NEW HAMPSHIRE FOUNDATION ACT

ARTICLE 21
Foreign Foundations

Section 564-F:21-2115

    564-F:21-2115 Automatic Cancellation upon Certain Mergers. – Upon merging into a foundation, a foreign foundation's registration automatically is cancelled effective upon the filing of the certificate of merger.

Source. 2017, 257:47, eff. Oct. 1, 2017.
