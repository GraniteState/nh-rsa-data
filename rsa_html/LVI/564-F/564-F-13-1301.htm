TITLE LVI
PROBATE COURTS AND DECEDENTS' ESTATES

CHAPTER 564-F
NEW HAMPSHIRE FOUNDATION ACT

ARTICLE 13
Protector's Duties

Section 564-F:13-1301

    564-F:13-1301 Duty to Act in Good Faith. – A protector has a duty to act in good faith, in accordance with the provisions of the governing documents, and in accordance with this chapter.

Source. 2017, 257:47, eff. Oct. 1, 2017.
