TITLE LVI
PROBATE COURTS AND DECEDENTS' ESTATES

CHAPTER 564-F
NEW HAMPSHIRE FOUNDATION ACT

ARTICLE 13
Protector's Duties

Section 564-F:13-1304

    564-F:13-1304 Duty to Maintain Records. – Unless the governing documents provide otherwise, a protector shall keep adequate records of the protector's acts as a protector.

Source. 2017, 257:47, eff. Oct. 1, 2017.
