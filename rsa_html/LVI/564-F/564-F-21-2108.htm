TITLE LVI
PROBATE COURTS AND DECEDENTS' ESTATES

CHAPTER 564-F
NEW HAMPSHIRE FOUNDATION ACT

ARTICLE 21
Foreign Foundations

Section 564-F:21-2108

    564-F:21-2108 Registered Office. –
(a) Each foreign foundation shall continuously maintain in this state a registered office.
(b) A foreign foundation's registered office may be the same as any of its offices in this state.

Source. 2017, 257:47, eff. Oct. 1, 2017.
