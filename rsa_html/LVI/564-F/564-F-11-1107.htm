TITLE LVI
PROBATE COURTS AND DECEDENTS' ESTATES

CHAPTER 564-F
NEW HAMPSHIRE FOUNDATION ACT

ARTICLE 11
Directors' Duties

Section 564-F:11-1107

    564-F:11-1107 Duty to Inform. – Unless the governing documents provide otherwise, a director has a duty to keep each qualified beneficiary reasonably informed about the foundation, the management of the foundation's affairs and property, and all material facts necessary for the beneficiary to protect the beneficiary's interests.

Source. 2017, 257:47, eff. Oct. 1, 2017.
