TITLE LVI
PROBATE COURTS AND DECEDENTS' ESTATES

CHAPTER 564-F
NEW HAMPSHIRE FOUNDATION ACT

ARTICLE 12
Protectors

Section 564-F:12-1204

    564-F:12-1204 Classes. –
(a) A foundation may have one or more classes of protectors.
(b) Each class of protectors shall have the duties and powers assigned to that class under the governing documents.

Source. 2017, 257:47, eff. Oct. 1, 2017.
