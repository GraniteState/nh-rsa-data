TITLE LVI
PROBATE COURTS AND DECEDENTS' ESTATES

CHAPTER 564-F
NEW HAMPSHIRE FOUNDATION ACT

ARTICLE 13
Protector's Duties

Section 564-F:13-1303

    564-F:13-1303 Duty of Impartiality. – Unless the governing documents provide otherwise, if a foundation has two or more beneficiaries, then the protector has a duty to act impartially in exercising his, her, or its powers, giving due regard to the foundation's purposes and the beneficiaries' interests.

Source. 2017, 257:47, eff. Oct. 1, 2017. 2018, 210:10, eff. June 8, 2018.
