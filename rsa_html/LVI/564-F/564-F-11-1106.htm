TITLE LVI
PROBATE COURTS AND DECEDENTS' ESTATES

CHAPTER 564-F
NEW HAMPSHIRE FOUNDATION ACT

ARTICLE 11
Directors' Duties

Section 564-F:11-1106

    564-F:11-1106 Duty to Maintain Records. – Unless the governing documents provide otherwise, a director shall keep adequate records of the foundation, the management of its property, and the management of its affairs.

Source. 2017, 257:47, eff. Oct. 1, 2017.
