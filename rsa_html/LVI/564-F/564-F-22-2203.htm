TITLE LVI
PROBATE COURTS AND DECEDENTS' ESTATES

CHAPTER 564-F
NEW HAMPSHIRE FOUNDATION ACT

ARTICLE 22
Reports and Other Filings

Section 564-F:22-2203

    564-F:22-2203 Penalty for Filing False Document. –
(a) A person commits an offense by:
(1) Filing with the secretary of state a document that the person knows is false in any material respect; or
(2) Signing a document that the person knows is false in any material respect with intent that the document be delivered to the secretary of state for filing.
(b) An offense under this section is a misdemeanor.

Source. 2017, 257:47, eff. Oct. 1, 2017.
