TITLE LVI
PROBATE COURTS AND DECEDENTS' ESTATES

CHAPTER 554-A
REVISED UNIFORM FIDUCIARY ACCESS TO DIGITAL ASSETS ACT

Section 554-A:17

    554-A:17 Uniformity of Application and Construction. – In applying and construing this chapter, consideration shall be given to the need to promote uniformity of the law with respect to its subject matter among states that enact the Revised Uniform Fiduciary Access to Digital Assets Act.

Source. 2019, 140:1, eff. June 25, 2019.
