TITLE XIII
ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES

CHAPTER 175
DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS

Section 175:5-c


[RSA 175:5-c effective January 1, 2027.]
    175:5-c Alcoholic Preparations Not Fit for Use as a Beverage. –
RSA 175 through RSA 180 shall not apply to the sale of alcoholic preparations that are not fit for use as a beverage, including:
I. Dietary supplements, as defined in 21 U.S.C. section 321, amended by the Dietary Supplement Health and Education Act of 1994, and as may be amended from time to time;
II. Nonprescription or proprietary medicines, as defined in RSA 318:1, XVIII;
III. Toilet, medicinal, or antiseptic preparations that are not fit for use as a beverage; and
IV. Flavoring extracts or syrups that are not fit for use as a beverage.

Source. 2026, 259:8, eff. Jan. 1, 2027.
