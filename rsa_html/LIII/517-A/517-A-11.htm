TITLE LIII
PROCEEDINGS IN COURT

CHAPTER 517-A
UNIFORM FOREIGN DEPOSITIONS LAW

Section 517-A:11


[RSA 517-A:11 effective January 1, 2027.]
    517-A:11 Commissioners Appointed by Foreign Jurisdictions. – A commissioner or other person appointed by any court of record of any other state, territory, or foreign country, for the purpose of taking depositions in this state for use in causes pending in such court of record, shall have the same powers of procuring the attendance of witnesses to give depositions before the commissioner and of requiring the production of papers and the giving of such depositions as justices of the peace within this state with reference to depositions for use in civil causes pending within the courts of this state.

Source. 2026, 172:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2027.
