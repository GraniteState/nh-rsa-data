TITLE LIII
PROCEEDINGS IN COURT

CHAPTER 517-A
UNIFORM FOREIGN DEPOSITIONS LAW

Section 517-A:4


[RSA 517-A:4 effective January 1, 2027.]
    517-A:4 Service of Subpoena. – A subpoena issued by a clerk of court under RSA 517-A:3 shall be served in compliance with all statutes and court rules governing service of subpoenas in civil actions in the superior courts.

Source. 2026, 172:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2027.
