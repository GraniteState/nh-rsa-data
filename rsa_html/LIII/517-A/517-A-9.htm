TITLE LIII
PROCEEDINGS IN COURT

CHAPTER 517-A
UNIFORM FOREIGN DEPOSITIONS LAW

Section 517-A:9


[RSA 517-A:9 effective January 1, 2027.]
    517-A:9 Procedure for Appointment of Commissioner. – After the appointment of a commissioner under RSA 517-A:8, the notice of the time and place of taking depositions before the commissioner, the proceedings in taking such depositions, the certificates to be made by the commissioner, and all other formalities with reference to taking, filing, and using such depositions shall be the same, as far as applicable, to taking depositions in civil causes.

Source. 2026, 172:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2027.
