TITLE LIII
PROCEEDINGS IN COURT

CHAPTER 517-A
UNIFORM FOREIGN DEPOSITIONS LAW

Section 517-A:5


[RSA 517-A:5 effective January 1, 2027.]
    517-A:5 Compliance with Subpoena. – All provisions of law and court rules regarding the obligation of persons to comply with subpoenas and the remedies available for non-compliance shall apply to subpoenas issued under RSA 517-A:3.

Source. 2026, 172:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2027.
