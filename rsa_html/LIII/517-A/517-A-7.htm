TITLE LIII
PROCEEDINGS IN COURT

CHAPTER 517-A
UNIFORM FOREIGN DEPOSITIONS LAW

Section 517-A:7


[RSA 517-A:7 effective January 1, 2027.]
    517-A:7 Uniformity of Application and Construction. – In applying and construing this uniform act, courts shall give due consideration to the need to promote uniformity of the law with respect to its subject matter among states that enact it.

Source. 2026, 172:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2027.
