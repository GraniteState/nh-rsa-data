TITLE LIII
PROCEEDINGS IN COURT

CHAPTER 525-A
CIVIL LEGAL ASSISTANCE

Section 525-A:2

    525-A:2 Civil Legal Services. – Funds appropriated to New Hampshire Legal Assistance shall be used to provide civil legal services to low-income persons in the state. These civil legal services shall include services related to such issues as housing, social security and other government benefits, health care, domestic violence, and consumer issues. New Hampshire Legal Assistance shall report semi-annually to the judicial council as to its use of these funds.

Source. 2005, 177:154, eff. July 1, 2005. 2007, 145:1, eff. July 1, 2007. 2019, 346:90, eff. July 1, 2019.
