TITLE LIII
PROCEEDINGS IN COURT

CHAPTER 525-A
CIVIL LEGAL ASSISTANCE

Section 525-A:1

    525-A:1 Statement of Purpose. – Funds appropriated to New Hampshire Legal Assistance for the purposes of civil legal assistance shall be used to enable the state to fund civil legal services to low-income persons in the state.

Source. 1997, 351:4, eff. July 1, 1997. 2019, 346:90, eff. July 1, 2019.
