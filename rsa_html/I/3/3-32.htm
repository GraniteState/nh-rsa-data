TITLE I
THE STATE AND ITS GOVERNMENT

CHAPTER 3
STATE EMBLEMS, FLAG, ETC.

Section 3:32

    3:32 State Marsupial. – The Virginia opossum (Didelphis virginiana) is hereby designated as the official state marsupial of New Hampshire and such opossums found within our borders shall be known as "New Hampshire opossums."

Source. 2025, 290:3, eff. Sept. 30, 2025.
