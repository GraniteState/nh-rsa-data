TITLE I
THE STATE AND ITS GOVERNMENT

CHAPTER 4
POWERS OF THE GOVERNOR AND COUNCIL IN CERTAIN CASES

Observances Proclaimed by Governor

Section 4:13-ff

    4:13-ff New Hampshire Day at the Big E. – The governor shall annually issue a proclamation observing the third Friday after Labor Day as "New Hampshire Day at The Big E" in recognition of the more than a century of support the Eastern States Exposition, Home of The Big E, has given to promoting agriculture and cooperation in the New England states.

Source. 2026, 229:3, eff. July 2, 2026.
