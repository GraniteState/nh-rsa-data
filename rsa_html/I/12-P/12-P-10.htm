TITLE I
THE STATE AND ITS GOVERNMENT

CHAPTER 12-P
DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY

Section 12-P:10

    12-P:10 Specific Answers. – The department may require any public utility or entity subject to its jurisdiction to provide documents and other materials and to make specific answers to questions upon which the department may need information within a time period set by the department.

Source. 2021, 91:187, eff. July 1, 2021. 2026, 4:4, eff. May 2, 2026.
