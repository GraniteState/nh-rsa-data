TITLE I
THE STATE AND ITS GOVERNMENT

CHAPTER 21-P
DEPARTMENT OF SAFETY

Homeland Security and Emergency Management

Section 21-P:43-a

    21-P:43-a Governor Duty. – The governor shall provide a list of all expenditures pursuant to this subdivision under a state of emergency to the executive council within 30 days of the expenditure, in order to ensure transparency.

Source. 2021, 91:461, eff. June 25, 2021. 2022, 56:1, eff. May 20, 2022.
