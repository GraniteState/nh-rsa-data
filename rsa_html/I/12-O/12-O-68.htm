TITLE I
THE STATE AND ITS GOVERNMENT

CHAPTER 12-O
DEPARTMENT OF BUSINESS AND ECONOMIC AFFAIRS

Wireless Deployment Initiative and Grant Fund

Section 12-O:68

    12-O:68 Federal Law Supersedes. – If any provision of this subdivision conflicts with any provision of funding guidance under federal law, the federal funding guidance shall supersede.

Source. 2022, 231:2, eff. Aug. 16, 2022.
