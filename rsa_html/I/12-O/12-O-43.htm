TITLE I
THE STATE AND ITS GOVERNMENT

CHAPTER 12-O
DEPARTMENT OF BUSINESS AND ECONOMIC AFFAIRS

New Hampshire Workforce Development

Section 12-O:43

    12-O:43 Workforce Development Director. – There is established within the office of workforce opportunity the position of director, which shall be an administrator IV position, classified at labor grade 33. The director shall lead the office of workforce opportunity. The director shall perform all duties which the commissioner may assign.

Source. 2017, 156:40, eff. July 1, 2017. 2023, 79:245, eff. July 1, 2023.
