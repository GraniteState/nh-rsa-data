TITLE I
THE STATE AND ITS GOVERNMENT

CHAPTER 5
DEPARTMENT OF STATE

Department and Secretary of State

Section 5:5-b

    5:5-b Copies of State Constitution to be Furnished. – The secretary of state is hereby directed to furnish such number of copies of the state constitution to the general public as he or she may deem necessary. The secretary of state may assess a fee to cover costs for bulk orders of copies of the state constitution requested by members of the legislative, judicial, and executive branches as well as members of the public.

Source. 2024, 237:1, eff. July 1, 2025.
