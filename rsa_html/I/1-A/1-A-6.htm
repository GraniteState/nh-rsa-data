TITLE I
THE STATE AND ITS GOVERNMENT

CHAPTER 1-A
NEW HAMPSHIRE COORDINATE SYSTEM

Section 1-A:6

    1-A:6 Restrictions. – The use of the terms "New Hampshire state plane coordinate system," "New Hampshire coordinate system 1927," or "New Hampshire coordinate system 1983" on any map, report of survey, or other document shall be limited to coordinates based on the respective New Hampshire coordinate system as defined in this chapter.

Source. 2026, 196:6, eff. June 19, 2026.
