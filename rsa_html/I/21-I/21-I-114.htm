TITLE I
THE STATE AND ITS GOVERNMENT

CHAPTER 21-I
DEPARTMENT OF ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES

Prohibition on Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion

Section 21-I:114

    21-I:114 Prohibition on DEI-Related Contract Provisions. – No agencies or political subdivisions shall enter into or renew any contract that includes DEI-related provisions, including requirements for contractors to implement DEI programs, conduct DEI training, or comply with DEI-related reporting obligations.

Source. 2025, 141:321, eff. July 1, 2025.
