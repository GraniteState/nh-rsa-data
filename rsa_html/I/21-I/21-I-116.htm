TITLE I
THE STATE AND ITS GOVERNMENT

CHAPTER 21-I
DEPARTMENT OF ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES

Prohibition on Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion

Section 21-I:116

    21-I:116 Review of Political Subdivision DEI-Related Contract Provisions. – The department of justice shall establish a process by which all political subdivisions review their existing contracts for the presence of DEI-related provisions.

Source. 2025, 141:321, eff. July 1, 2025.
