TITLE I
THE STATE AND ITS GOVERNMENT

CHAPTER 21-I
DEPARTMENT OF ADMINISTRATIVE SERVICES

Prohibition on Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion

Section 21-I:113

    21-I:113 Prohibition on DEI Initiatives. – No public entity shall implement, promote, or otherwise engage in any DEI-related initiatives, programs, training, or policies. No state funds shall be expended for DEI-related activities, including but not limited to implicit bias training, DEI assessments, critical race theory, or race-based hiring, promotion, or contracting preferences.

Source. 2025, 141:321, eff. July 1, 2025.
