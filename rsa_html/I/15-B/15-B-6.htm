TITLE I
THE STATE AND ITS GOVERNMENT

CHAPTER 15-B
GIFTS, HONORARIUMS, AND EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENTS

Section 15-B:6

    15-B:6 Duty to Report. – An elected official, public official, or public employee who receives an, honorarium or expense reimbursement shall file a report with the secretary of state no later than the last day of the month following the month during which the honorarium or expense reimbursement was received.

Source. 2006, 21:9. 2007, 354:6, eff. Sept. 15, 2007. 2016, 328:14, eff. Dec. 7, 2016.
