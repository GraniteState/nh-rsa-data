TITLE I
THE STATE AND ITS GOVERNMENT

CHAPTER 21-H
DEPARTMENT OF CORRECTIONS

Section 21-H:17

    21-H:17 Department of Corrections; New Hampshire State Prison Fund Established. – There is hereby established in the state treasury a state prison fund, which shall be administered by the department of corrections. The fund shall not lapse. The fund is established for the purpose of design and construction of a new New Hampshire state prison.

Source. 2023, 79:48, eff. June 30, 2023.
