TITLE XLII
NOTARIES, COMMISSIONERS, JUSTICES OF THE PEACE, AND ACKNOWLEDGMENTS

CHAPTER 456-B
UNIFORM LAW ON NOTARIAL ACTS

Section 456-B:8-e

    456-B:8-e Relation to Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act. – This chapter modifies, limits, and supersedes the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act, 15 U.S.C. section 7001 et seq., but does not modify, limit, or supersede section 101(c) of that act, 15 U.S.C. section 7001(c), or authorize electronic delivery of any of the notices described in section 103(b) of that act, 15 U.S.C. section 7003(b).

Source. 2021, 206:2, Pt. II, Sec. 8, eff. Feb. 6, 2022.
