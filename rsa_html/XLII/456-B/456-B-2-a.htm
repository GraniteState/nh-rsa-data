TITLE XLII
NOTARIES, COMMISSIONERS, JUSTICES OF THE PEACE, AND ACKNOWLEDGMENTS

CHAPTER 456-B
UNIFORM LAW ON NOTARIAL ACTS

Section 456-B:2-a

    456-B:2-a Personal Appearance Required. – If a notarial act relates to a statement made in or a signature executed on a record, the individual making the statement or executing the signature shall appear personally before the notarial officer.

Source. 2021, 206:2, Pt. II, Sec. 5, eff. Feb. 6, 2022.
