TITLE XXIV
GAMES, AMUSEMENTS, AND ATHLETIC EXHIBITIONS

CHAPTER 287-I
SPORTS BETTING

Section 287-I:11

    287-I:11 Disclosure of Data Source. – The commission and agents shall publicly disclose the source of the data that will be used to determine the outcome of a tier II or tier III wager.

Source. 2019, 215:1, eff. July 12, 2019.
