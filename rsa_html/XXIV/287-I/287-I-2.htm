TITLE XXIV
GAMES, AMUSEMENTS, AND ATHLETIC EXHIBITIONS

CHAPTER 287-I
SPORTS BETTING

Section 287-I:2

    287-I:2 Sports Book Authorized. – The commission is authorized to operate a sports book for the purposes of accepting and paying sports wagers by authorized bettors within the state in conformance with the requirements of this chapter.

Source. 2019, 215:1, eff. July 12, 2019.
