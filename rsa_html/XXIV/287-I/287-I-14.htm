TITLE XXIV
GAMES, AMUSEMENTS, AND ATHLETIC EXHIBITIONS

CHAPTER 287-I
SPORTS BETTING

Section 287-I:14

    287-I:14 Financial Reports. – The commission may seek financial and compliance reports from its agents periodically and may conduct audits of these reports to ensure that the state receives the agreed upon revenue sharing proceeds.

Source. 2019, 215:1, eff. July 12, 2019.
