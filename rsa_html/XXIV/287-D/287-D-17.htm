TITLE XXIV
GAMES, AMUSEMENTS, AND ATHLETIC EXHIBITIONS

CHAPTER 287-D
GAMES OF CHANCE

Section 287-D:17

    287-D:17 Equipment; Inspection. –
I. All devices and equipment used to conduct games of chance shall be subject to inspection by duly authorized law enforcement or lottery commission officials.
II. No mechanism or device, which can be used to regulate odds, shall be permitted to operate games of chance.

Source. 2014, 329:1. 2015, 276:127, eff. July 1, 2015. 2023, 109:11, eff. July 1, 2023.
