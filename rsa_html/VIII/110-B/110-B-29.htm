TITLE VIII
PUBLIC DEFENSE AND VETERANS' AFFAIRS

CHAPTER 110-B
DEPARTMENT OF MILITARY AFFAIRS AND VETERANS SERVICES

Public Property

Section 110-B:29

    110-B:29 Use of Armories or Other National Guard Facilities. – All New Hampshire national guard facilities shall be primarily for the military duty, instruction, and training of the national and state guard and for the storage and maintenance of military property. Other use of national guard facilities may be authorized by the adjutant general and shall be governed by rules and regulations promulgated under this section.

Source. 1981, 434:1. 2002, 248:8, eff. May 17, 2002. 2021, 91:369, eff. July 1, 2021; 207:2, Pt. VII, Sec. 5, eff. July 1, 2021.
