TITLE XXI
MOTOR VEHICLES

CHAPTER 261
CERTIFICATES OF TITLE AND REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES

Number Plates

Section 261:91-b


[RSA 261:91-b repealed by 2025, 126:2, effective July 4, 2027.]
    261:91-b 250th Anniversary of American Independence Commemorative License Plates. – The director is authorized to adopt rules pursuant to RSA 541-A for the design, sale, and distribution by the department of safety celebrating the 250th anniversary of American Independence, and the display of said plates in place of the front license plate on vehicles, during which the celebration is taking place. The funds and revenues raised under this section shall be deposited in the land and community heritage investment program administrative fund under RSA 227-M:7-a. The price for the plates shall be $25, and they shall be furnished by the department of safety.

Source. 2025, 126:1, eff. Aug. 23, 2025.
