TITLE XXI
MOTOR VEHICLES

CHAPTER 266-A
MOTOR CARRIERS OF PASSENGERS

Section 266-A:9

    266-A:9 Applications for Certificates and Permits. –
Applications for certificates and permits required by RSA 266-A:4 and RSA 266-A:7 shall be:
I. Made in writing to the department;
II. Verified under oath;
III. Accompanied by the proper fee;
IV. In the form specified in rules adopted by the commissioner pursuant to RSA 541-A; and
V. With the information specified in rules adopted by the commissioner pursuant to RSA 541-A.

Source. 1941, 224:9. RL 304:9. RSA 376:9. 1981, 435:29. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(7). 2024, 33:14, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 266-A:9.
