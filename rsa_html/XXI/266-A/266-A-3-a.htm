TITLE XXI
MOTOR VEHICLES

CHAPTER 266-A
MOTOR CARRIERS OF PASSENGERS

Section 266-A:3-a

    266-A:3-a Certification as Vanpool Operator. – Each person, upon being certified by the director of the division of motor vehicles of the department of safety as a vanpool operator, shall provide said director with a certificate indicating that the vehicle used in vanpooling service is covered by a liability insurance policy in such amount as may be prescribed by the director.

Source. 1977, 382:3, eff. Sept. 3, 1977. RSA 376:3-a. 2024, 33:14, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 266-A:3-a.
