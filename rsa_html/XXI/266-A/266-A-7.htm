TITLE XXI
MOTOR VEHICLES

CHAPTER 266-A
MOTOR CARRIERS OF PASSENGERS

Section 266-A:7

    266-A:7 Contract Carrier Permit Required. – No person shall operate a motor vehicle as a contract carrier of passengers between points in this state without a permit issued by the department.

Source. 1941, 224:7. RL 304:7. RSA 376:7. 1981, 435:27. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(7). 2024, 33:14, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 266-A:7.
