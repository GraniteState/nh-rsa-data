TITLE XXI
MOTOR VEHICLES

CHAPTER 266-A
MOTOR CARRIERS OF PASSENGERS

Section 266-A:5

    266-A:5 Issuance of Common Carrier Certificate. –
I. A certificate authorizing, in whole or in part, an operation for which application is made shall be issued to any qualified applicant.
II. Conditions for qualifications:
(a) The applicant must be fit, willing, and able to properly perform the proposed service;
(b) The applicant must agree to conform to the provisions of RSA 266-A and any rules adopted by the commissioner relative to RSA 266-A; and
(c) The proposed service must be required by the present or future public convenience.

Source. 1941, 224:5. RL 304:5. RSA 376:5. 1981, 435:25. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(7). 2024, 33:14, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 266-A:5.
