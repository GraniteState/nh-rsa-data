TITLE XXI
MOTOR VEHICLES

CHAPTER 266-A
MOTOR CARRIERS OF PASSENGERS

Section 266-A:12

    266-A:12 Transfer of Certificates and Permits. – No certificate or permit, nor any rights thereunder, shall be transferred without the approval of the department.

Source. 1941, 224:12. RL 304:12. RSA 376:12. 1985, 402:6, I(e)(7). 2024, 33:14, eff. July 1, 2024. RSA 266-A:12.
