TITLE XLI
LIENS

CHAPTER 451-C
SELF-SERVICE STORAGE FACILITY LIENS

Section 451-C:3

    451-C:3 Entry of Owner Into Rented Space. – An owner may enter a rented space without notice to the renter in the event of an emergency or to provide services that are reasonably necessary or were agreed to by the renter.

Source. 2019, 242:1, eff. Oct. 10, 2019.
