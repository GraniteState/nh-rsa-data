TITLE XLI
LIENS

CHAPTER 451-C
SELF-SERVICE STORAGE FACILITY LIENS

Section 451-C:13

    451-C:13 Purchaser. – A purchaser in good faith of personal property under the provisions of this chapter shall take the personal property free and clear of any other's rights, even if the owner has not complied with the provisions of this chapter or the rental agreement.

Source. 2019, 242:1, eff. Oct. 10, 2019.
