TITLE LX
CORRECTION AND PUNISHMENT

CHAPTER 622
THE STATE PRISONS

General Provisions

Section 622:9

    622:9 Mittimus; Return. – The clerk of the court shall deliver a copy of the conviction, judgment and order, either electronically or in hand, issued pursuant to RSA 622:8 to the commissioner of corrections or designee and to the arresting municipality. The department of corrections shall examine the order to ensure its authenticity and to confirm, before placing a person in the state department of corrections' custody, that the order provides due authority to the department of corrections to confine said person.

Source. RS 227:8. CS 242:8. GS 270:8. GL 288:8. PS 285:9. PL 400:10. RL 464:10. 2024, 14:2, eff. Jan. 1, 2025.
