TITLE LIX
PROCEEDINGS IN CRIMINAL CASES

CHAPTER 604-A
ADEQUATE REPRESENTATION FOR INDIGENT DEFENDANTS IN CRIMINAL CASES

Section 604-A:9

    604-A:9 Repayment. – As of July 1, 2025, all collections efforts under this section or former versions of this chapter shall be terminated. Any person subject to a court order for reimbursement may petition the court to vacate the reimbursement order.
