TITLE LIX
PROCEEDINGS IN CRIMINAL CASES

CHAPTER 598-A
PROFESSIONAL BONDSMEN

Section 598-A:1-a

    598-A:1-a Fees. – A person proposing to become bail or surety and registered as a professional bondsman shall pay to the executive director of the office of professional licensure and certification an initial application fee. The fee shall be deposited in the office of professional licensure and certification fund.

Source. 1977, 386:2. 1989, 8:2. 1992, 288:55, eff. July 1, 1992. 2023, 79:338, eff. Sept. 1, 2023.
