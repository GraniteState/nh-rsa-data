TITLE II
COUNTIES

CHAPTER 28-B
COUNTY-STATE FINANCE COMMISSION

Section 28-B:1

    28-B:1 Commission Established. –
There is hereby established the county-state finance commission which shall consist of the following members:
I. The commissioner of the department of health and human services or designee.
II. The director of the division of long term supports and services in the department of health and human services.
III. Three members appointed by the commissioner of the department of health and human services, and one member appointed by the governor and council, who shall serve for a term of 2 years and shall continue to serve until their successors are appointed.
IV. Six members representing county government, all appointed by the New Hampshire Association of Counties, who shall serve for a term of 2 years and shall continue to serve until their successors are appointed.
V. Three members of the legislature, 2 of whom shall be members of the house of representatives, one of whom shall be a member of the house finance committee and one other member of the house of representatives, appointed by the speaker of the house of representatives, and one of whom shall be a member of the senate, appointed by the president of the senate, whose terms shall be coterminous with their terms in office.

Source. 2003, 223:2, eff. July 1, 2003. 2009, 188:1, eff. Sept. 11, 2009. 2022, 323:30, eff. Sept. 6, 2022. 2026, 30:1, eff. June 21, 2026.
