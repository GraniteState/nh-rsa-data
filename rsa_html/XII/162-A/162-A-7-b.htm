TITLE XII
PUBLIC SAFETY AND WELFARE

CHAPTER 162-A
BUSINESS FINANCE AUTHORITY

Section 162-A:7-b


[RSA 162-A:7-b repealed by 2023, 79:593, effective June 30, 2033.]
    162-A:7-b Annual Grants to Regional Economic Development Corporations. – The sum of $200,000 is hereby appropriated annually to the authority for the purpose of providing equal grants to regional economic development corporations in furtherance of the objectives set forth in RSA 162-A:1. The governor is authorized to draw a warrant for said sum out of any money in the treasury not otherwise appropriated. Funds appropriated to the authority under this section shall be excluded from the repayment provisions of RSA 162-A:30.

Source. 2023, 79:592, eff. July 1, 2023.
