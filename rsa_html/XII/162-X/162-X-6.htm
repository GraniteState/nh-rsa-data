TITLE XII
PUBLIC SAFETY AND WELFARE

CHAPTER 162-X
SPECIAL ASSESSMENT DISTRICTS

Section 162-X:6


[RSA 162-X:6 effective September 13, 2026.]
    162-X:6 Appeals. – Any property owner subject to an assessment may appeal the apportionment of costs to the governing body within 30 days of notice, and thereafter to the superior court pursuant to RSA 677.

Source. 2026, 329:3, eff. Sept. 13, 2026.
