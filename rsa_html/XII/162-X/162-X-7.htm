TITLE XII
PUBLIC SAFETY AND WELFARE

CHAPTER 162-X
SPECIAL ASSESSMENT DISTRICTS

Section 162-X:7


[RSA 162-X:7 effective September 13, 2026.]
    162-X:7 Dissolution. – A district shall dissolve upon repayment of all obligations or earlier by vote of the governing body, provided all costs have been satisfied.

Source. 2026, 329:3, eff. Sept. 13, 2026.
