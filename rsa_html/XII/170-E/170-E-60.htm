TITLE XII
PUBLIC SAFETY AND WELFARE

CHAPTER 170-E
CHILD DAY CARE, RESIDENTIAL CARE, AND CHILD-PLACING AGENCIES

Recreation Camp Licensing

Section 170-E:60

    170-E:60 Use of Epinephrine. – Immediately after using epinephrine, the child shall report such use to the nurse or another camp employee to enable the nurse or camp employee to provide appropriate follow-up care.

Source. 2019, 346:133, eff. Jan. 1, 2020. 2025, 204:1, eff. July 1, 2025.
