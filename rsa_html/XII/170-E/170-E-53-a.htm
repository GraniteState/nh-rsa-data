TITLE XII
PUBLIC SAFETY AND WELFARE

CHAPTER 170-E
CHILD DAY CARE, RESIDENTIAL CARE, AND CHILD-PLACING AGENCIES

Recreation Camp Licensing

Section 170-E:53-a

    170-E:53-a Purpose. – The purpose of this subdivision is to provide for the licensing of recreation camps and certification of criminal background checks for youth skill camps.

Source. 2019, 346:133, eff. Jan. 1, 2020.
