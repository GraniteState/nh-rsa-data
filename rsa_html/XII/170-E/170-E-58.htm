TITLE XII
PUBLIC SAFETY AND WELFARE

CHAPTER 170-E
CHILD DAY CARE, RESIDENTIAL CARE, AND CHILD-PLACING AGENCIES

Recreation Camp Licensing

Section 170-E:58

    170-E:58 Statement of Health for Recreational Camps. – Notwithstanding any law or rule to the contrary, any physical examination which is required before a child may enter a recreational camp may be conducted by a physician, an advance practice registered nurse, or a physician associate.

Source. 2019, 346:133, eff. Jan. 1, 2020. 2025, 105:1, eff. June 17, 2025.
