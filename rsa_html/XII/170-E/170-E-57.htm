TITLE XII
PUBLIC SAFETY AND WELFARE

CHAPTER 170-E
CHILD DAY CARE, RESIDENTIAL CARE, AND CHILD-PLACING AGENCIES

Recreation Camp Licensing

Section 170-E:57

    170-E:57 Recreation Camp and Youth Skill Camp Fund. – There is established the recreation camp and youth skills camp fund. This fund shall be nonlapsing and continually appropriated to the commissioner of the department of health and human services, for the purpose of paying costs associated with administering the provisions of this subdivision.

Source. 2019, 346:133, eff. Jan. 1, 2020.
