TITLE XII
PUBLIC SAFETY AND WELFARE

CHAPTER 170-E
CHILD DAY CARE, RESIDENTIAL CARE, AND CHILD-PLACING AGENCIES

Recreation Camp Licensing

Section 170-E:61

    170-E:61 Availability of Epinephrine. – The recreational camp nurse or, if a nurse is not assigned to the camp, the recreational camp administrator shall maintain for the use of a child with severe allergies at least one dose of epinephrine, provided by the child or the child's parent or guardian, which shall be readily accessible to the recreational camp staff caring for children requiring such medications.

Source. 2019, 346:133, eff. Jan. 1, 2020. 2021, 122:50, eff. July 9, 2021. 2025, 204:1, eff. July 1, 2025.
