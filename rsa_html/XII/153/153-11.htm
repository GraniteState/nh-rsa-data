TITLE XII
PUBLIC SAFETY AND WELFARE

CHAPTER 153
STATE BOARD OF FIRE CONTROL

Section 153:11

    153:11 Report of Fire Department Incident Responses. – Every fire department incident response occurring in this state shall be reported to the state fire marshal within 10 days after the occurrence of the same by the appropriate fire chief or designee. The state fire marshal shall provide the necessary instructional data for the fire chiefs for incident reporting. Notwithstanding RSA 21-G:9, II(b), the state fire marshal may adopt rules pursuant to RSA 541-A with the approval of the commissioner of the department of safety relative to fire department incident response reporting.

Source. 1947, 251:1, par. 9. RSA 153:11. 1957, 59:1. 1971, 115:1; 486:8. 1975, 442:8, eff. Aug. 19, 1975. 2026, 104:2, eff. July 27, 2026.
