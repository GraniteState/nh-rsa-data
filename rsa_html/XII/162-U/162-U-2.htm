TITLE XII
PUBLIC SAFETY AND WELFARE

CHAPTER 162-U
COOS COUNTY ESTABLISHED AS DISTRESSED PLACE-BASED ECONOMY

Section 162-U:2

    162-U:2 Regulatory Principle for Department Commissioners. – [RSA 162-U:2 repealed by 2025, 141:331, effective July 1, 2045.]
The general court hereby declares that Coos County is a distressed place-based economy. All agencies shall consider this declaration in decisions affecting Coos county.

Source. 2025, 141:330, eff. Aug. 26, 2025.
