TITLE XII
PUBLIC SAFETY AND WELFARE

CHAPTER 168-B
PARENTAGE

Section 168-B:20

    168-B:20 Rulemaking. – The department of health and human services may adopt rules, pursuant to RSA 541-A, to carry out its duties under this chapter.

Source. 2014, 248:2, eff. July 21, 2014. 2023, 105:13, eff. Aug. 19, 2023.
