TITLE XII
PUBLIC SAFETY AND WELFARE

CHAPTER 168-B
PARENTAGE

Section 168-B:13

    168-B:13 Marriage or Partnership of a Gestational Carrier During the Gestational Carrier Arrangement. – The marriage, partnership, separation, or divorce of any party to a gestational carrier agreement does not affect the validity or the terms of the gestational carrier agreement.

Source. 2014, 248:2, eff. July 21, 2014. 2023, 105:11, eff. Aug. 19, 2023.
