TITLE XII
PUBLIC SAFETY AND WELFARE

CHAPTER 168-B
PARENTAGE

Section 168-B:4

    168-B:4 Substantial Compliance. – Substantial compliance with the requirements of this chapter is sufficient to allow for a determination of parentage under this chapter.

Source. 2014, 248:2, eff. July 21, 2014. 2023, 105:6, eff. Aug. 19, 2023.
