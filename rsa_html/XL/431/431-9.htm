TITLE XL
AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE AND ANIMAL HUSBANDRY

CHAPTER 431
SOIL AND PLANT ADDITIVES

Fertilizers

Section 431:9

    431:9 Commercial Value. – For the purpose of determining the commercial value to be applied under the provisions of RSA 431:8, the commissioner shall determine the value of the deficient lot based on the invoiced price.

Source. 1985, 72:1. 1995, 233:1, eff. Jan. 1, 1996. 2023, 82:5, eff. Aug. 19, 2023.
