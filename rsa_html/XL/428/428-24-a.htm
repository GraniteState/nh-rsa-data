TITLE XL
AGRICULTURE, HORTICULTURE AND ANIMAL HUSBANDRY

CHAPTER 428
POULTRY AND POULTRY PRODUCTS

Sale of Eggs

Section 428:24-a

    428:24-a Exemption for Certain Producers. – Producers who sell exclusively from their residence, farm store or stand, or to local stores and/or establishment shall label their eggs in accordance with RSA 428:23, but otherwise shall not be subject to RSA 428:22 through RSA 428:24 and RSA 428:25 through RSA 428:29.

Source. 2026, 119:1, eff. Aug. 4, 2026.
