TITLE L
WATER MANAGEMENT AND PROTECTION

CHAPTER 485-A
WATER POLLUTION AND WASTE DISPOSAL

Cyanobacteria Mitigation Loan Program

Section 485-A:60

    485-A:60 Rules. – The department of environmental services shall adopt rules, under RSA 541-A, relative to administering cyanobacteria loan and grant programs for eligible projects.

Source. 2023, 79:433, eff. July 1, 2023.
