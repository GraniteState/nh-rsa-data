TITLE L
WATER MANAGEMENT AND PROTECTION

CHAPTER 482-B
NEW HAMPSHIRE WATER WELL BOARD

Section 482-B:14


[RSA 482-B:14 effective until January 1, 2027; see also RSA 482-B:14 set out below.]
    482-B:14 Fees. – Fees received under this chapter shall be paid over to the state treasurer and deposited in the general fund.
[RSA 482-B:14 effective January 1, 2027; see also RSA 482-B:14 above.]
    482-B:14 Fees. – Fees received under this chapter shall be paid over to the state treasurer and deposited in the water well board fund established in RSA 482-B:4-a.

Source. 1989, 339:1, eff. Jan. 1, 1990. 2026, 290:3, eff. Jan. 1, 2027.
