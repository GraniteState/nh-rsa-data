TITLE L
WATER MANAGEMENT AND PROTECTION

CHAPTER 483-F
PCB ASSISTANCE

Section 483-F:2

    483-F:2 Definitions. –
In this chapter:
I. "Department" means the department of environmental services.
II. "Fund" means the PCB assistance fund established in this chapter.

Source. 2023, 79:36, eff. July 1, 2023.
