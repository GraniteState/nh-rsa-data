TITLE L
WATER MANAGEMENT AND PROTECTION

CHAPTER 485-C
GROUNDWATER PROTECTION ACT

Section 485-C:18

    485-C:18 Administrative Fines. – The commissioner may impose an administrative fine not to exceed $4,000 for each offense upon any person who violates any provision of this chapter or any rule, permit or order adopted or issued under this chapter. Each day of violation shall constitute a separate offense. Appeals from a decision of the commissioner under this section shall be in accordance with RSA 21-O:7, IV. Any administrative fine imposed under this section shall not preclude the imposition of further penalties under this chapter. The commissioner shall adopt rules, under RSA 541-A, relative to a schedule of administrative fines which may be imposed under this section for violation of this chapter.

Source. 1991, 344:1, eff. July 1, 1993. 2019, 263:8, eff. Jan. 1, 2020. 2026, 185:33, eff. Aug. 18, 2026.
