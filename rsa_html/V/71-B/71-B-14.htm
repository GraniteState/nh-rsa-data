TITLE V
TAXATION

CHAPTER 71-B
BOARD OF TAX AND LAND APPEALS

Section 71-B:14

    71-B:14 Staff. – The board shall have such clerical and technical staff as may be necessary within the limits of appropriation made therefor.

Source. 1973, 544:2. 2003, 63:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2004. 2019, 346:198, eff. July 1, 2019. 2025, 141:30, eff. July 1, 2025.
