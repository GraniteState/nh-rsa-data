TITLE V
TAXATION

CHAPTER 79
FOREST CONSERVATION AND TAXATION

Carbon Sequestration Programs Study Commission

Section 79:38

    79:38 Public Land Enrollment in Timber Carbon Sequestration Programs Prohibited. – No fee simple ownership lands owned by the state or by a county shall be enrolled in a timber carbon sequestration program.

Source. 2026, 51:1, eff. July 7, 2026.
