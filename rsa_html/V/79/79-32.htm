TITLE V
TAXATION

CHAPTER 79
FOREST CONSERVATION AND TAXATION

Carbon Sequestration Programs Study Commission

Section 79:32


[RSA 79:32 repealed by 2025, 164:4, II, effective November 1, 2027.]
    79:32 Carbon Sequestration Programs Study Commission. – There is established a commission to study the impacts of the growing practice of dedicating tracts of New Hampshire forests to sequestration of carbon emissions.

Source. 2025, 164:3, eff. July 1, 2025.
