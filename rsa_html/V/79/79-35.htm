TITLE V
TAXATION

CHAPTER 79
FOREST CONSERVATION AND TAXATION

Carbon Sequestration Programs Study Commission

Section 79:35


[RSA 79:35 repealed by 2025, 164:4, II, effective November 1, 2027.]
    79:35 Chairperson; Quorum. – The members of the study commission shall elect a chairperson from among the members. The first meeting of the commission shall be called by the senate member. The first meeting of the commission shall be held before September 15, 2025. Six members of the commission shall constitute a quorum.

Source. 2025, 164:3, eff. July 1, 2025.
