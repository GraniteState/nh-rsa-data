TITLE V
TAXATION

CHAPTER 78
TOBACCO TAX

Enforcement

Section 78:22

    78:22 Reinstatement After Revocation. – The commissioner may reinstate a revoked tobacco tax certificate if he or she is satisfied that the former certificate holder will comply with the provisions of this chapter.

Source. 2019, 178:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2020. 2025, 224:15, eff. July 1, 2025.
