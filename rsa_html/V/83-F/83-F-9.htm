TITLE V
TAXATION

CHAPTER 83-F
UTILITY PROPERTY TAX

Section 83-F:9


[RSA 83-F:9 effective until July 1, 2027; see also RSA 83-F:9 set out below.]
    83-F:9 Exemption From Education Tax. – Persons and property subject to taxation under this chapter shall not be subject to tax under RSA 76:3; provided, however, that nothing in this chapter shall be construed to exempt such persons or property from local school, municipal, district, or county taxation under RSA 76.
[RSA 83-F:9 effective July 1, 2027; see also RSA 83-F:9 above.]
    83-F:9 Exemption From Education Tax. – Persons and property subject to taxation under this chapter shall not be subject to tax under RSA 76:3; provided, however, that nothing in this chapter shall be construed to exempt such persons or property from local school, municipal, district, or county taxation under RSA 76, except as provided in RSA 72:74, VIII.

Source. 1999, 17:35; 303:8. 2005, 257:3. 2008, 173:15, eff. July 1, 2009. 2025, 206:4, eff. July 1, 2027.
