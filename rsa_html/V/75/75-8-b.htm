TITLE V
TAXATION

CHAPTER 75
APPRAISAL OF TAXABLE PROPERTY

Section 75:8-b


[RSA 75:8-b effective July 1, 2026; see also RSA 75:8-b above.]
    75:8-b Annual Appraisal; Municipalities Over 10,000. – Except when assessing real estate under RSA 75:8-a, any municipality with a population over 10,000 as determined pursuant to RSA 78-A:25 intending to appraise real estate annually at market value, as defined in RSA 75:1, shall authorize such annual appraisal by a majority vote of the governing body. The governing body shall hold 2 public hearings regarding the annual appraisal process at least 15 days, but not more than 60 days, prior to the governing body's authorization vote. Any municipality with a population over 10,000 as determined pursuant to RSA 78-A:25 annually appraising real estate at market value shall provide notification of changes to the assessed valuation at least 45 days prior to the issuance of the final tax bill. Such notice shall be by public notice in a local newspaper of general circulation, by public notice on the municipalities' main website or any social media accounts utilized by the municipality, and public notice posted in the 2 places where the municipality regularly posts notices of its governing body meetings, or by any other means deemed appropriate by the governing body.

Source. 2004, 203:15, eff. June 11, 2004. 2025, 265:4, eff. July 1, 2026.
