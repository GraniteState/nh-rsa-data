TITLE V
TAXATION

CHAPTER 78-E
PROTECTION OF PRIVATE CUSTOMER INFORMATION AND RIGHTS OF NEW HAMPSHIRE REMOTE SELLERS IN CONNECTION WITH CERTAIN FOREIGN SALES AND USE TAXES

Section 78-E:10

    78-E:10 Annual Reports. – On or before November 1 of each year, the department of justice shall report on the status of implementation of the provisions of RSA 78-E along with any recommendations for proposed legislation to the president of the senate, the speaker of the house of representatives, the chairs of the senate and house committees on ways and means, the senate clerk, the house clerk, the governor, and the state library.

Source. 2019, 280:1, eff. July 19, 2019.
