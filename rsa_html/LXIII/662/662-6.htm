TITLE LXIII
ELECTIONS

CHAPTER 662
ELECTIVE DISTRICTS

Section 662:6

    662:6 Delegates to State Party Conventions. – At every state primary election, the voters shall elect delegates to each state party convention in the same manner and in the same proportion as state representative districts.

Source. 1979, 436:1. 1982, 29:2; 43:8. 1983, 248:5; 282:2; 317:2; 424:6. 1985, 31:2. 1992, 183:8. 2002, 28:1. 2012, 63:1, eff. May 14, 2012. 2022, 10:1, eff. Mar. 23, 2022.
