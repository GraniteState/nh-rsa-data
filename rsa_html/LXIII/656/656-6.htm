TITLE LXIII
ELECTIONS

CHAPTER 656
PREPARATION OF VOTING MATERIALS

State General Election

Section 656:6

    656:6 Designation of Office. – Immediately to the left of the set of party columns shall be an offices column which shall list the offices, each preceded by the word "For," for which the candidates whose names are listed in the party columns have been nominated, as in "For Governor." Below the appropriate phrase shall be printed in small but easily legible letters:
I. "Vote for not more than 1" (if there is only one office to be filled, such as the governor or state senator); or
II. "Vote for up to X" (insert the number of offices to be filled) "X will be elected" (insert the number of offices to be filled).

Source. 1979, 436:1. 1986, 126:3. 1994, 309:2; 324:1. 2004, 201:2. 2005, 62:1, eff. July 22, 2005. 2023, 43:1, eff. July 18, 2023.
