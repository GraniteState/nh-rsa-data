TITLE LXIII
ELECTIONS

CHAPTER 659
ELECTION PROCEDURE

Challenges

Section 659:32

    659:32 Record by Clerk. – The town clerk shall record the name and domicile of all challenged voters, the name and domicile of the person who challenged each such voter, the reason for each challenge, and the ruling on each challenge by the moderator or election official.

Source. 1979, 436:1, eff. July 1, 1979. 2024, 378:8, eff. Nov. 11, 2024.
