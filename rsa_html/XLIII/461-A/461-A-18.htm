TITLE XLIII
DOMESTIC RELATIONS

CHAPTER 461-A
PARENTAL RIGHTS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

Section 461-A:18

    461-A:18 Repayment. – In any case where a guardian ad litem has been appointed pursuant to RSA 461-A:16 and the responsible party's proportional share of the expense was ordered to be paid by the judicial council from the prior special fund established pursuant to RSA 461-A:17, which resulted in a court order for reimbursement, said order may be vacated upon petition to the court.

Source. 2005, 273:1. 2011, 224:70, eff. July 1, 2011. 2023, 107:3, eff. Aug. 22, 2023. 2025, 141:219, eff. July 1, 2025.
