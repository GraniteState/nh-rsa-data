TITLE XLIII
DOMESTIC RELATIONS

CHAPTER 461-B
EMANCIPATION

Section 461-B:1

    461-B:1 Definitions. –
In this chapter:
I. "Minor" means a person who is under 18 years of age.
II. "Court" means the judicial branch family division, unless otherwise indicated.

Source. 2019, 110:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2020.
