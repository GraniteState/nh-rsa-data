TITLE XLIII
DOMESTIC RELATIONS

CHAPTER 457
MARRIAGES

Age

Section 457:4

    457:4 Marriageable. – No person below the age of 18 years shall be capable of contracting a valid marriage, and all marriages contracted by such persons shall be null and void.

Source. 1907, 80:1. PL 286:4. RL 338:4. 2009, 59:2, eff. Jan. 1, 2010. 2018, 272:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2019. 2024, 81:1, eff. Jan. 1, 2025.
